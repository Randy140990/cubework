Deepkit 0.1.2 for Minecraft 1.21.11 (Fabric; needs Fabric API). Lamps redone.

- Lamp beams are now drawn by your game: a soft round pool of warm light thrown onto whatever the lamp points at, bright in the middle, fading at the edge and with distance, with a little spill round you. It follows your head or hand smoothly every frame (no more jumping a block at a time), looks the same with and without shaders, and you see other players' lamps too.
- Lamps no longer put anything in the world, so gravel and sand stay put. Flares still light the area round them as before, but quietly: they never make gravel or sand fall either.
- The headlamp is worn as a strap round the head with the lamp on the forehead, and the chest lamp as a harness with the lamp on the chest, instead of the item sitting on your head.

Deepkit 0.1.1 for Minecraft 1.21.11 (Fabric; needs Fabric API). Fixes from the first test.

- The headlamp and chest lamp are worn: right-click one (or put it in the helmet or chestplate slot), then press H to switch it on. Fitting one onto a helmet or chestplate in a crafting grid still works, to keep your armour.
- A lamp that's on shimmers like an enchanted item, so you can see it's on in daylight, and switching one says so above the hotbar with its battery.
- H with no lamp worn switches a hand torch you're holding, or says how to put a lamp on.

Deepkit 0.1.0 for Minecraft 1.21.11 (Fabric; needs Fabric API). First build, for testing.

- Hand torch, headlamp and chest lamp. The hand torch switches with use, in either hand. The headlamp and chest lamp fit onto a helmet or chestplate in a crafting grid (and come off the same way), and switch with H. Battery bar; charge with glowstone dust (+50%) or redstone (+20%) in a crafting grid; flickers below 10%.
- Beams light where they land (and faintly halfway), for everyone, with or without shaders. The light is invisible and cleans itself up.
- Flares in red, green, blue and white: use to strike, use again to throw, sneak and use to drop. 60 seconds, bright light, sparks and smoke, works underwater, never starts fires.
- Glow paint in 8 colours (64 marks a can): use on a block to paint, use in the air to choose the mark (arrow, cross, dot, circle, !, ?, tick, 0 to 9). Arrows: on floors and ceilings they point the way you face; on walls, to the edge you click nearest. Cleaned with a sponge or by breaking.
- Rope coil: use on the edge of a drop to hang 32 blocks of climbable rope; use on a rope's bottom to add more; sneak and use (or break it) to take it back.
- Grappling hook: hold use and let go to fire (24 blocks); it catches the top edge of a block and drops a rope to you. Hold use on a rope to winch up. Sneak and use on its rope to take it back.
