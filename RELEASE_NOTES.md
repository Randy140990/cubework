Orbit Arena by Inside the Cube (Minecraft 1.21.11, Fabric).

0.1.0: the first arena.
- PvP Arena on the pause menu opens the setup: who you fight (fighters, mob waves or a boss), difficulty 1 to 10, how many (up to 100; bosses up to 3), your kit, a time limit and how full the crowd is. Start fades you into the arena; your own things are put away and given back after (even if the game closes mid-fight).
- The arena: its own space dimension. A round stadium sunk into an asteroid, with a honeycomb floor of glowing lines, a lit centre dais, two pedestals, two decks of stands, an orange band with floodlights, four boxes, tunnels, and a roof ring edged with light strips, under a night sky with the Milky Way that turns once every 20 minutes. Floating rocks all round.
- Matches: 3, 2, 1, Fight!; a bar with how many are left and the time; enemies come out in waves until all have come. Win by beating them all; lose by going down (you don't die), running out of time, or leaving (Leave Arena on the pause menu). Results in chat.
- Fighters fight like players, better as the difficulty rises: strafing, shield blocks, jump crits, axe swaps against your shield, sprint resets, mace leaps, spears, golden apples; armour from leather (1) to netherite with Protection IV (10).
- Mob waves add tougher mobs as the difficulty rises (up to brutes, wither skeletons, evokers, breezes and ravagers), with more health and harder hits. Bosses: the Wither, the Warden, the Ender Dragon (experimental).
- The crowd fills the stands (on your screen only, so it doesn't load the server): astronauts, robots, critters and supporters, some with signs. They cheer your hits and kills, go wild when you win, and groan when you take a big hit.
- An invisible barrier keeps the fight on the floor and below the roof. The arena can't be broken or built on (except in creative), and it's rebuilt after mob and boss fights.

Sky: Night Sky HDRI 002 from ambientCG (CC0).
