Retromon UI Viewer

Every screen the game lays over the world, from switching on to battling, buying and trading. Step through each one as you would in game. They're drawn in the mod's own look, after Let's Go: white panels with a pearly rim, words in dark ink, names in coral, sunny yellow for what's picked, every screen on its own bright backdrop.

The game's words follow Minecraft's own language (Options, Language); the picker above shows them. Names and words are the old games' for now (Pokédex, Prof. Oak, Poké Ball); they'll be swapped for Retromon's own later. The creatures are Retromon's own models from the Creature Viewer, the world is the Interior Viewer's, and every icon and panel is drawn from scratch.
