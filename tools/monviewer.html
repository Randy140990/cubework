Retromon Creature Viewer

Retromon's own creatures beside the Cobblemon models they stand in for, at their in-game sizes, animated as the mod plays them. Drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom.

Grid squares are 1 block.
Left: the Cobblemon model (by the Cobblemon team, CC BY-NC), shown for comparison. Right: Retromon's own, made from scratch with its own textures and animations. Both are the same species underneath: the stats, moves and levelling below belong to the species and don't change with the look.
