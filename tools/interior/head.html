Retromon Interior Viewer

Walk the insides of Retromon's buildings with the camera the game uses inside (north up, 52° down, field of view 34, held on the whole room; it closes in with the black bars when someone talks), the near wall left off. Walls, floors and carpets are our own textures drawn at Minecraft's 16 pixels to a block, so what you see here is what goes in the game; you move as the game moves you (0.6 wide, 4.3 blocks a second, 5.6 running, steps up to 0.6) and A picks who to talk to the way the mod does. Pick any creature to follow you at its true size. Small ones can ride on your shoulder.
