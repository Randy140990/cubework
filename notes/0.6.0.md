# Retromon 0.6.0: 3D people and Pokémon on screens, Let's Go battle bars, hop-in attacks (18 Nov 2026)

`retromon-0.6.0+1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.5.0. The Kanto map jar (`retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar`) is unchanged.
Saves carry on. Compiled in CI; not run here.

## 3D figures on screens
- New: blocky 3D figures drawn straight onto screens (no world needed): people on Minecraft's player model,
  Pokémon on their own models, idling and moving (`client/Figures.java`, one picture per frame).
- Intro: Oak stands on the right, his feet on the top of the text box (the text starts on the left), turned
  towards it, a hand out and nodding as he talks. You and your rival appear the same way.
- BOY / GIRL: both in 3D; the one you point at turns to you, hops and waves, with a smiley or heart bubble.
- Naming: whoever's being named stands in 3D beside the card. While the map copies, you wave.

## Starter
- Oak no longer gives his speech again as the game starts. As in the games, he stops you when you head out
  of Pallet Town without a Pokémon ("Hey! Wait! Don't go out!"), you're put back a few steps inside the town,
  and he lets you choose. (On a map without Pallet Town marked, his welcome comes straight away, as before.)
- Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle stand on their cards in 3D on coloured pads; the one you point at hops
  twice (twirling on the first), cries, and gets a heart bubble. After you choose, it hops beside Oak.

## Battle
- The way in: two quick flashes, then a slanted sweep of white from left to right with streaks running ahead,
  a hold on white while the stage goes up, then the white fades.
- Framing: your Pokémon low and left of centre, clear of your card; theirs off to the right. No grass, flowers
  or trees between the camera and the Pokémon.
- Cinema frame: the top and bottom darken smoothly into the picture and fade in by opacity (no hard bars).
  Conversations use the same.
- Cards: slanted dark plates, smaller (yours no longer covers your Pokémon): name, gender, Lv, status, HP;
  yours with HP numbers and EXP.
- Commands: full coloured slanted bars (FIGHT red, POKéMON green, BAG yellow, RUN blue) with icons; the picked
  one slides out with a white edge.
- Moves: full bars in the move's type colour, the type on a white tag, the name, PP (yellow when low, red at 0).
  The panel on the left: type, PHYSICAL / SPECIAL / STATUS, power, accuracy, and how it'll do against theirs
  (Super effective, Not very effective, No effect).
- Attacks: both sides now hop in. Moves that hit up close: draw back, spring in with a hop, land, trot back
  (the hit lands as it arrives). Moves from afar: rear back and hop where it stands. The camera no longer
  swings round behind theirs; it just pushes in a touch.
- Effects: special moves gather light on the attacker and stream across in their colour, bursting on the
  target; claws and blades slash, the rest thump; on every hit a star of light bursts on the screen over the
  one hit (rays and a ring, bigger when super effective) and the move's colour glows round the screen's edges
  (no full-screen tint). Stat changes show rising (warm) or falling (blue) arrows and a spiral.
- A catch: the battle music stops for the caught jingle and stays off for the Pokédex entry.

## Pokédex entry (first catch)
- Seen on the Pokédex itself: the red case with its lens and lights; on its screen the Pokémon in 3D turning
  on a stand, a scan line as it comes in, REGISTERED, then the number, name, kind, types, height, weight and
  the entry.
