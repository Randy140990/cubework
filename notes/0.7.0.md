# Retromon 0.7.0: characters, Oak's lab and your rival, catching, the Pokédex voice, nights (18 Nov 2026)

`retromon-0.7.0+1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.6.0 (about 30 MB now, with the Pokédex voice). The map jar is unchanged.
Start a NEW GAME to see the new start. Compiled in CI; not run here.

## Characters
- New skins for Oak, the boy, the girl and the rival, using both skin layers (`tools/heroes.py`):
  - **Oak:** spiky swept-back silver hair, a lab coat with tails, a red shirt.
  - **Boy:** a red and white cap, dark hair under it, a navy jacket with teal trim over a black tee, a yellow backpack, charcoal jeans and trainers.
  - **Girl:** a white sun hat with a red band, long brown hair, a blue top, a red skirt and white socks.
  - **Rival:** big spiky hair and a purple shirt with a pendant.
- In the intro:
  - Oak's head follows the pointer up and down as well as across.
  - The one you point at does a different thing each time: waves, cheers, jumps for joy, points, or spins. Each gets its own bubble.
  - Whoever is being named thinks while you type.
- The title has a little twinkling star over the last O.

## The start, as in the games
- Oak stops you as you step into the path out of Pallet Town, at the tree line. You're put back where you last stood in town (no more clipping behind fences), then the screen fades and you're at his lab's door.
- Oak no longer stands at the edge of town. He comes when you try to leave.
- The starters do their own cry animation when you point at them.
- After you name your first Pokémon, your rival takes the one strong against yours and battles you. Losing doesn't count against you (Oak heals you).

## Battles
- **The time of day is real** (night battles at night). The world's clock stands still during a battle, so there are no jumps before, during or after.
- **Unseen lights** over the stage make night battles moonlit rather than dark.
- **Catching:** the ball bounces up off the Pokémon and opens. The Pokémon turns to red light and streams into it, the ball shuts and drops, then shakes. If it breaks free, the Pokémon pops back out.
- **Animations:**
  - Pokémon play Cobblemon's own animations where they have them: a cry when they come out, a flinch when hit, a cry for status moves.
  - Hidden parts are now hidden. Pidgey's open wings were showing on top of its closed ones, which is what made it look wrong. Portraits were redrawn too.
- **The Pokédex after a catch:** white fades in and out of it. You hear the Pokémon's cry, then the Pokédex reads the entry aloud in a robotic voice, like the TV Pokédex.
- **Bag:** two pockets (POKé BALLS, MEDICINE), switched with left/right or the tabs. Each item shows on a bar with its picture and count, and the picked item's description (from the games) is on a card beside the bag.
- **POKéMON:** a column of slanted plates (picture, name, level, HP, status, IN BATTLE). The picked one is shown in 3D on a stand with its types and moves.
- **Your card:** the blue strip under it is gone (it looked like an EXP bar). The only bar under HP is EXP.

## Around the world
- **Nights:** a blue, moonlit night you can see in, rather than pitch black. This works with shader packs too (it uses the night vision value they read). OPTION > NIGHTS: DARK, SOFT (default), BRIGHT.
- **Lamps light further:** around every lamp in a place (sea lanterns, lanterns, glowstone and so on), unseen light blocks are set in two rings. This is done once per place when its people are put in, so new games get it.
- **People stand at street level:** no more nurse on the Pokémon Center roof (new games).
- **Signs are solid**, and you can read them with SPACE. A shelf standing in for a sign gives the place's sign ("PALLET TOWN: Shades of your journey await!").
- **When you're behind a building or a tree,** your outline shows through it in sky blue.
- **HUD (bottom right):** the time with a sun or moon, plus SPACE Talk, ENTER Menu, R Speed.
- **START > WAIT:** rest until morning, midday, evening or night. The screen fades to black while time passes.
- **Conversations:** the top and bottom shading now fades in and out, and the text box rises into place.
- **OPTION:** text speed, music, POKéDEX VOICE (on/off), NIGHTS.
- **Pokédex:** V or LISTEN reads an entry you've caught.
