# Retromon 0.65.0: the gyms' battles on the TV show's battlefield (2026-11-25)

John's 09:28: "all gym battles ... look to be on a battle field from the tv show. I.e. symbol on the ground etc. but
must be surrounded in the theme of that gym - I.e pewter would be rocky etc". Map jar unchanged (Retromon-Map-1.0.0).

## Every gym's hall is now the show's battlefield
- **The field** runs down the middle of the hall (11 blocks across, 15 long), as the show's:
  - white lines round it and across its middle;
  - the big centre circle with the POKé BALL in it, its red half towards them;
  - a trainer's box at each end, round where each trainer stands.
- **Everyone stands where they did** in the halls: theirs and their trainer at the far end, yours at the near end, the
  camera behind yours looking up the field. So the battle's framed as before.
- **Its surface and its surroundings are the gym's own:**
  - **PEWTER (BROCK):** rocky earth with pebbles and cracks. Sand round it, rough rock walls, big slabs of rock and tan
    boulders down each side, BROCK's stone stage at the back, the stone RHYHORN.
  - **CERULEAN (MISTY):** the pool is the field. The POKéMON stand on white platforms floating on it. The big centre
    platform has the circle and the POKé BALL, and each trainer's box is its own platform. Lane ropes, the diving board
    at the back, stands down both sides.
  - **VERMILION (LT. SURGE):** riveted steel plates with a yellow and black hazard rim. Generators along the back, coils
    glowing down each side, floodlights in the corners.
  - **CELADON (ERIKA):** a lawn mown in stripes, under the glass. The gym's hedges down each side, flower beds, trees in
    the back corners.
  - **FUCHSIA (KOGA):** polished boards up the field, tatami either side. Paper screens and dark posts, red lanterns,
    scrolls at the back.
  - **SAFFRON (SABRINA):** dark violet polished tiles. The gym's glowing warp pads down each side, crystal-topped
    pillars, crystals glowing in the air.
  - **CINNABAR (BLAINE):** dark volcanic rock with glowing cracks. A channel of lava down each side, lava falling down
    the back wall, ember-lit rocks and braziers.
  - **VIRIDIAN (GIOVANNI):** red-brown earth with a gold rim. Gold-capped pillars down each side, his dais at the
    back, the statues.
- **The ELITE FOUR and the CHAMPION too,** the same field in the LEAGUE's halls (gold rim, gold-capped pillars down
  each side):
  - LORELEI: frosted ice, snow drifts, icicles;
  - BRUNO: packed sand, boulders, punching bags;
  - AGATHA: dark violet stone, drapes, gravestones;
  - LANCE: teal stone, braziers, banners;
  - the CHAMPION: white marble with gold lines, the red runner, red drapes, trophies.
- **The other halls** (the Lab, the Dojo, the buildings) are as they were.
