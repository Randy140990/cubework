# Retromon 0.57.0: the review, Pallet to Cinnabar and all of Johto (John's 15:17 to 16:35) (2026-11-23)

The map jar is unchanged (Retromon-Map-1.0.0). One fix is made to the map itself, in the game: the Dark Cave's way in
on Route 46 is raised to 3 high, as John asked (see Route 46 below).

## Everywhere
- **Doors:** every door is redrawn crisp, in each wood's and metal's own colour (bamboo first). Each has a frame, two inset panels, a window above and a brass handle; the metal doors are riveted with a small window.
- **Shelves used as trims** are drawn as plain flat boards in their colour, with no lip and no recess. That covers chimney caps, the bands under windows, planters and bridge rails. Signs stay signs.
- **Lamps:**
  - the caps are one plain colour, with no pattern;
  - the feet are plain, with a single band;
  - the copper lanterns everywhere are the garden lights.
- **Rocks:** every rock the scans found is retextured, in the water as the sea rocks and on land as the boulders. That covers the joins between routes, the Whirl Islands' chains, Cherrygrove's bay, Olivine's harbour, Cianwood's shore, Routes 19 to 21 and 47 to 48, and Route 28.
- **Cobblestone and mossy cobblestone:** the moss runs onto the cobblestone in soft tufts (Route 1, Route 2, everywhere).
- **Gyms:** the dark middle column at every Gym's door is a clean dark panel.
- **Cave mouths:** the hanging dripstone is clean grey stone points. Diglett's Cave's mouths have their stone pillars again.
- **Stands:** every plant-pot stand is alike, with dark timber posts and frame, a wooden counter and a slatted roof with no fire showing.
- **Planters:** the cherry saplings are a round flowering shrub.
- **Banners (16:35):**
  - two banners one above the other are one long banner;
  - each plain banner has a design of its own, in gold and white;
  - the cords between the posts are dark and the posts' tops gold.
- **Lily pads:** they no longer cast a shadow, which showed through the water as a second pad (Retromon Light 1.2.8).
