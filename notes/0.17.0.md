# Retromon 0.17.0: real flames, eyes by nature, detailed faces, call effects (19 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.17.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.16.0. `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` goes beside it, unchanged. Compiled in CI; not run here.

This pass strengthens the fourteen POKéMON LOOK: RETROMON models from the 0.16.0 feedback. The designs stay Retromon's own: no move towards the originals.

## Fire that looks like fire
- **What changed:** the ember goats' flames used to be stacked blocks. They are now flames: crossed flat parts, so they look full from every side.
- **How they look:** each has a white-hot core, then yellow, orange, and red at the edges and tips. The tongues lick up and sway over six frames, ten a second, glowing in the dark.
- **Where:**
  - **Cinderkid:** a fringe and a tail flame.
  - **Emberam:** a fringe, a mane of two flames down the neck, and a tail flame.
  - **Pyribex:** a fringe, a mane of three flames down the neck, and a great tail flame.
- **On a call:** the flames still flare up.

## Eyes by nature
Hunters and fighters look forward; prey watches round the sides.

| Eyes | Who | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front, with brows | Cinderkid, Emberam, Pyribex, Brambadillo, Grovadon, Tsunapir | On the face. The goats have amber eyes: round on the kid, narrowed on Emberam, fierce on Pyribex. Brows angle down over each. |
| Corner | Fernadillo, Tidapir, Surfapir, the quails, the jerboas | Round the corner of the face, so they show face-on and from the side. |

Every eye has a highlight, a pupil, an iris lit underneath, whites where they fit, and a blink.

## Faces
- **Flat faces:** each head now has a flat face the full width across, so the eyes sit on it.
- **Mouths:** nostrils and mouths on every muzzle. The jaws open when they call, showing the inside of the mouth.
- **By family:**
  - **Fernadillo:** blush cheeks.
  - **Brambadillo:** violet thorn cheeks.
  - **Grovadon:** mossy brows.
  - **Pyribex:** fangs.
  - **The jerboas:** a front tooth and a mouth.
  - **The tapirs:** a paler muzzle round the trunk.

## Effects when they call
- **The tapirs:** spray a jet of water from the trunk. It widens away from the trunk, white at its heart and pale blue at its edges, and its streaks flow outward and break into drops. This is animated.
- **The armadillos:** throw a crown of leaves up from their backs, turning as it rises. Grovadon's is the biggest.
- **The rest of the time:** both are hidden, including when hit.

## Fixes
- **Flat parts (eyes, lids):** they have nothing behind their face, so their back face is left clear. Before, the front and back sat in the same place and could fight over which was drawn.
- **Heads with flat faces:** these are built from three boxes that no longer overlap.

Its texel counts include parts that only show glowing, such as flames and water.

## Tools
- **`tools/monlib.py`:**
  - `flame()` and `jet()`, with their animated painters;
  - `face_blob()`;
  - corner eyes, and `brows()`;
  - the seam handling for joins between boxes;
  - `outward()` for flat parts.
- **`tools/mons.py`:**
  - face painters (`smile`, `nostrils`, `open_mouth`, `muzzle_patch`);
  - effect tracks (`hidden`, `burst`, `spray`).
- **`tools/monshow.py`:** draws glowing-only parts, frame by frame.
- **`tools/monviewer.py`:** counts glowing-only texels.
