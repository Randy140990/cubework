## CERULEAN CAVE (16:36: "for integrated")
- Your three floors, 1F, 2F and B1F, stitched as FireRed has them: 1F's six ladders up to 2F, and one down to B1F.
- **The way in:** the mouth on the river's north bank. You walk north up its little passage into the dark; you come out
  where you went in.
- **FireRed's way through is by SURF**, which the mod hasn't yet. So in here (only here) you can walk into the water and
  swim it.
- **Its look:** FireRed's pale grey speckled floor; your mossy cliffs green, moss on top; pale stone steps; the
  red-brown rock round the water; its rocks grey boulders (2F's maze is walls of them, as FireRed's).
- **MEWTWO** waits in the room on B1F, at level 70.
- FireRed's items on all three floors.
- **ROCK SMASH isn't one of this Kanto's moves** (FireRed only gives it on the Sevii Islands), and 2F's cracked rocks
  shut off all three of its items. So 2F's cracked rocks are taken out. 1F's and B1F's stay: they block nothing.

## VICTORY ROAD (16:36)
- Your three floors, 1F, 2F and 3F, stitched as FireRed's:
  - in by Route 23's west mouth onto 1F;
  - up to 2F, up to 3F;
  - back down to 2F's east side, where 2F's little alcove is the way out by the east mouth.

  east side to the INDIGO PLATEAU.
- **Its look:** FireRed's olive-grey earth floor and yellow sand; red-brown rock walls in ledges, the mountain's pale rock
  on top; steps cut in the rock; the switches in the floor as round plates; its rocks and boulders warm granite.
- **Trainers:** FireRed's thirteen, in FireRed's places with FireRed's names (COOLTRAINER ROLANDO, BLACK BELT DAISUKE
  and the rest). RAY and TYRA are a double battle in FireRed; there are no double battles yet, so they battle you
  one at a time.

- **The rock barriers:** FireRed's three (one each floor) open when a boulder is pushed onto a switch, and boulders can't
  be pushed yet. They stay as you built them, drawn as boulders, and the switches as round plates: in your build every
  floor's way through goes round them, so they shut nothing off.
