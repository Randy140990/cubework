# Retromon 0.19.0: battle animations, moves, jointed legs, 13 new creatures (19 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.19.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.18.0. `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` goes beside it, unchanged. Compiled in CI; not run here.

## Battle animations (every Retromon creature)

### Actions
These play over whatever the creature is doing:

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Attack** | A wind-up, a lunge with the head driving in, and the jaws (or beak) snapping. |
| **Its move** | It gathers itself with a crouch and a shiver, then lets the move go with the head up and the mouth open. The move's effect flies. |
| **Critical** | A bigger lunge, a jagged white-and-gold impact burst where it lands, and "A critical hit!" with a flash in the viewer. |
| **Hit** | Knocked back with a flinch and the eyes squeezed shut. Press it while **Confused** to see a creature hurt itself. |
| **Recall** | In the viewer, a red beam from a ball tints and shrinks it in, then sends it back out. |

### Poses
- **Confused:** stars circle over its head, its eyes go to rings, and it sways and lolls.
- **Faint:** a stagger, then over onto its side. It rests on the ground rather than sinking into it, limp, eyes crossed out, and stays down until recalled.

### In the game
The animations are in the model files, so battles can use them. Today the game still plays only the call and hit; triggering attack, move, critical, confused and faint from battles is the next step.

## Models
- **Flames:**
  - Crisp pixel-art teardrops on two crossed planes: narrow at the base, swelling, then licking tongues.
  - A pale heart, yellow, orange, and a red-orange rim, with no dark edges.
  - Kept upright on the tails (turned back against the tail's tilt) with a wisp of smoke.
  - Pyribex's tail flame is bigger.
- **Joints:**
  - Armadillos and tapirs: a thick upper leg, a knee cap (front legs) or hock (back legs), a slimmer lower leg and a broad foot with toes or claws, set half out from the body.
  - Goats: muscled haunches, knee caps and hocks, slimmer shins.
- **Birds:** the quails have feathered thighs down to a scaly ankle, and raptor feet with three splayed toes, a hind toe and hooked talons. Regalquail has bushy feathered brows.
- **Jerboas:** long toed feet with claws, a fluffy brush of a tail tuft, and Jerbolt has meaner eyes.
- **Calls:** longer jaws hinge far back and drop wide, showing the inside of the mouth.

- **Where they come from:** Route 22 (Nidoran, Mankey, Spearow) and Viridian Forest (Pikachu, Pichu by breeding).
- **Viridian Forest:** isn't built yet, so the gliders are viewer-only until it is. The encounter table is ready.
- **Detail:** above Cobblemon's for 12 of the 13. Galeshrike is within 16%.
- **Sizes:** matched by height. Ours are four-legged, so most run longer than Cobblemon's upright models.

## Viewer
- **Families:** five more, 27 creatures in all.

- **Evolutions:** shown by stone and friendship as well as by level.
- **Types:** Ground, Fighting and Electric types added.
