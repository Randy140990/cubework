# Retromon 0.58.0: Cinnabar's insides, the Sprout Tower, the Academy, the National Park's gates, the Ilex Forest (2026-11-23)

The map jar is unchanged (Retromon-Map-1.0.0). This covers the interiors you passed over (15:52, 16:15 and 16:23), the
Ilex Forest you sent at 17:07, and the swings (17:09).

## Cinnabar Island (FireRed's rooms, a tile a block)
- **The Gym:** in through the glass doors either side of the dark middle column.
  - Its rooms are behind green walls, with a long lavender pipe down the middle and Blaine on his purple rug at the top left.
  - **The quiz:** each of the six gates is shut until you answer its machine's question right. Get one wrong and that room's trainer comes for you. Beating them opens the gate too, as in FireRed. Beating Blaine opens every gate.
  - The trainers are the first games' teams; the Gym Guide stands by the door.
- **The Pokémon Lab:** in through its door.
  - **The hall:** the reception counter, the glass table, and three doors along the hall: the Meeting Room, the R&D Room and the Testing Room.
  - **Fossils:** the scientist in the Testing Room brings your Helix Fossil, Dome Fossil or Old Amber back to life.
  - **Trades:** FireRed's three.
    - In the Meeting Room: an Electrode (ESPHERE) for a Raichu and a Tangela (TANGENY) for a Venonat.
    - In the Testing Room: a Seel (SEELOR) for a Ponyta.
- **The Pokémon Mansion:** all four floors.
  - It has pink brick walls with a cream face, the rubble and rocks, white pillars along the pink runner, arched windows, the stairs, and 3F's holes, which drop you to the floor below.
  - **The secret switch:** each floor's statue has the switch. Pressing it swaps the barred gates on every floor: the shut ones open and the open ones shut.
  - The items are the first games', plus FireRed's Protein and HP Up. The hidden items are found by looking at the spot. The diaries can be read.

## Violet City and the National Park (Crystal's rooms, a step a block)
- **The Sprout Tower:** 1F to 3F.
  - It has wide boards, log rails, the great swaying pillar up the middle and the ladders between floors.
  - The Sages battle with Crystal's teams: Chow, Nico, Edmond, Jin, Troy and Neal.
  - Elder Li is at the top, in front of the painting.
- **Earl's Pokémon Academy:** in through the bamboo doors of the house with the chimney. It has the blackboard, the bookshelves, the rows of desks, Earl and his pupils, and the notebook.
- **The National Park's gates:**
  - **Route 35's:** in from the route at the south. The way out at the north goes to the park. If you come in by the east part's north doorway, you go back out the way you came.
  - **Route 36's:** in from the route at the east, out at the west to the park.
  - Each gate has its counter and its officer. The contest-day people aren't in yet.

## The Ilex Forest (17:07)
- Your build is a room of its own, as Viridian Forest is.
- **Azalea Town's gate:** its doorway on the town side takes you in, in front of the south gatehouse. Its steps take you back out.
- **The north gatehouse:** its steps take you out of Route 34's Ilex Forest gate, towards Goldenrod. That gate's doorway brings you back in.
- **What's placed:** HeartGold's people stand where it has them: the charcoal maker's apprentice, his lost Farfetch'd, the charcoal maker and the Headbutt man. Its four item balls are out, and its nine hidden items are found by looking at the spot.
- **The rest:** the sign and the shrine can be read. The light green patches are its tall grass. It's dim under the trees.
