## Riding the MAGNET TRAIN

- **The stations' insides.** Walk in under either station's roof (the doorway up its steps) and you're in its hall.
  Both are laid out as HeartGold's:
  - the waiting hall: its seats, its flower beds, its ticket machines;
  - the rail across the hall, with the ticket gate in the middle and the OFFICER standing at it;
  - past the gate, the platform, with the train waiting at it.
  - **GOLDENROD's** hall is in Johto's teal and gold. Its PRESIDENT is in it: "My dream was to build a train that is
    faster than any POKéMON."
  - **SAFFRON's** hall is in Saffron's green and yellow. Its GYM GUIDE and a LASS are in it.
- **The ride.** Talk to the OFFICER: "We'll soon depart for SAFFRON. Are you coming aboard?" Say YES ("Right this way,
  please") and:
  - the screen fades, then you're watching the train at the station from outside, at the game's usual angle, with the
    cutscene bars in;
  - after a moment it pulls out along your line, faster and faster, and into the tunnel at the end;
  - the MAGNET TRAIN's own tune plays while you ride;
  - the screen fades again, and at the other city the train comes out of that tunnel, slows along the line and pulls
    in under the station's roof;
  - a last fade and you're inside that station by the gate. Its OFFICER says "We have arrived in SAFFRON. We hope to
    see you again."
- **The train is our own design.** It's one long white car with a nose at each end, so it can run either way. It has a
  dark band of windows, a deep blue stripe with a gold line along its sides, a pale grey roof, and two lamps on each
  nose.
- **No PASS needed yet.** For now anyone can ride. Once we've settled the story (your idea of a Kanto champion
  starting again in Johto), I can make it need the rail PASS, as HeartGold does.
