# Retromon 0.24.0: the rest of the UI Viewer's screens (19 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.24.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.23.0. `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` goes beside it, unchanged. Compiled in CI; not run here.

This jar brings in every UI Viewer page that 0.23.0 didn't have. Each one is laid out as the viewer lays it out, so you can check them side by side.

## Pokédex (START menu > POKéDEX)
- **The list:** the viewer's blue page.
  - SEEN and CAUGHT are in the header.
  - Nine rows at a time, each with its number, picture and name, and a ball by the ones you've caught.
  - Ones you've only seen show as a dark shape. The rest show as "?????".
  - The one you're on turns on a stand at the right. Its name, types and "Stands in for #025 PIKACHU" sit beneath it (with Cobblemon's look, its number and kind).
  - Up and down move one row; left and right move a page; TAB shows only the ones you've caught.
  - The list is Kanto's 151 until you've seen one from further afield.
- **The entry (A):** the creature on its stand beside the entry panel. The panel has:
  - its number, name and types;
  - Height in blocks, and what it stands in for (Weight with Cobblemon's look);
  - what it's like, and where to find it.
  - V reads it out, A shows where it lives on the Town Map, and up and down go to the next one you've seen.
- **Your first catch of a kind:** the same entry page, with "Added to your POKéDEX!".

## Places
- **Healing desk:** the nurse's lines from the viewer. When you say YES, your POKéMON go in and the ♫ ♪ notes play for a few seconds, then "Your POKéMON are fighting fit!".
- **Shop:** the viewer's shop. Its parts:
  - the clerk's text box;
  - MONEY at the top left;
  - the price list on the right;
  - "In your bag" for the item you're on;
  - the How many? panel.
- **Getting an item:** the item held up in a card with its notes.
- **Getting a badge:** the badge spinning in with the light behind it, then its notice on the HUD.
- **Trading (new):** the four trades from the old games now ask for their POKéMON:
  - ABRA for MR. MIME on Route 2;
  - POLIWHIRL for JYNX in Cerulean;
  - SPEAROW for FARFETCH'D in Vermilion;
  - SLOWBRO for LICKITUNG on Route 18.
  - The trade looks like the viewer's: You send and You get cards, TRADE or CANCEL, then the two balls cross over between the stands.
  - Without the POKéMON they want, they tell you to come back.
- **Being given a POKéMON, and nicknames:** "Do you want to give a nickname to ...?" with it held up on its stand against the warm light, as in the viewer.

## Battle
- **Gotcha!** pops up when you catch one.
- **Levelling up:**
  - "grew to Lv. 6!" comes with the viewer's stats panel at the right: each stat's new value, and what it went up by in green.
  - A new move: "... wants to learn the move ...", YES or NO, then the viewer's list of moves with their types. Pick one to forget, or the new one to not learn it.
- **Evolving:** the viewer's page.
  - "What? ... is evolving!"
  - It stops turning and glows white, pulsing, with "Press ESC to stop it".
  - It's gone in the light, then back as its new form.
  - "Congratulations!" pops up.
- **Winning:** "You defeated ...!", then the prize money counting up in the viewer's MONEY box.
- **Losing:** "has no POKéMON left that can fight!", the money dropped, everything fades to white, "... ... ...", "whited out!". You wake where you were last healed (or at home), inside or out.

## START menu
- **SAVE:** your save card (player, place, badges, Pokédex, time) above "Would you like to save the game?". Saving shows "Saving... Don't turn off the power." with the disc spinning.
- **WAIT:** the viewer's dim with "Time passes...", then "It's morning now." (or midday, evening, night).
- **OPTION:** the viewer's rows. New ones:
  - **SOUND:** how loud cries and menu sounds are.
  - **BATTLE EFFECTS:** move animations on or off.
  - **CONTROLS:** opens the key map.

## Controls
- **The keys page (new):** a new game now starts with "These are your keys": Use these keys, Show the key map, or Back.
- **The key map (new):**
  - the whole keyboard, each key in the colour of what it does: in game, in menus, for building, Minecraft's own, turned off, or still to decide;
  - the key you're on described below it;
  - what else Retromon turns off.

## Not in this jar
- **LANGUAGE:** the game's words are only in English so far, so it isn't in OPTION yet.
- **BATTLE STYLE:** asking whether to switch when a trainer sends out the next one isn't in yet.
- **The title's OPTIONS:** this still opens Minecraft's options. Retromon's are in the START menu once you're playing.
