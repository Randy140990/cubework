# Retromon 0.69.0: the Ruins of Alph, between its two gates (John's 26 Nov 15:58)

John's words: "we've got the ruins of Alf to incorporate within the 2 gates ... you would go into them. Those gates
obviously need to be open. I think people can go in and just walk around and explore it, but I would say none of
those caves or anything will have any interaction until I build that."

## Getting there

The ruins aren't built into John's world. They're a place of their own, the way Ilex Forest is. His schematic goes in
block for block: all 148 by 192 of it, with the chambers, the research centre, the pond and the channel, his signs,
his two gatehouses and the trees seven deep all round.

- **From Route 36.** Go into the Ruins of Alph gate and walk out its south side. You come out on the path at the foot
  of the ruins' north gatehouse, facing into the ruins.
- **From Route 32.** Go into the gate by the lake and walk out its west side. You come out at the foot of the ruins'
  east gatehouse, facing west.
- **Going back.** Walk up either gatehouse's steps into its doorway. You're back in that gate, then out onto its
  route. You can go in by one gate and out by the other.
- **The gatehouses' doorways.** From inside the ruins they have daylight at their end, the same as the forests'
  gatehouses.
- **The chambers' doorways** stay John's black, with nothing in them. None of the caves does anything yet.

## Walking round

- **What's there.** Nothing is locked and nobody is there. There are no wild POKéMON (there's no tall grass), no
  puzzles and no items. It's just the ruins to walk round. You can't walk into the water.
- **The signs.** John's three double shelf signs are the game's sign, and each one reads as HeartGold has it:
  - by the path down from the Route 36 gatehouse: "RUINS OF ALPH. Visitors who are interested in archaeology are
    welcome.";
  - below the middle chamber: "RUINS OF ALPH. Feel free to explore the ruins and become a fossil professor!";
  - beside the research centre's door: "RUINS RESEARCH CENTER".
- **The place pill** says RUINS OF ALPH.

## How it looks

John's blocks there are in the same looks as his blocks out on Johto:

- **Johto's looks everywhere:** the beach sand with the grass's tufts onto it, the cliff rock, the trees' bark and
  leaves, the glass, the quartz and the trims.
- **His rock:** the chambers' rough courses, the mounds, the rocks and the rubble are the boulders' stone, the same as
  his rocks and the Whirl Islands mounds.
- **The gatehouses** have his Johto gates' looks.
- **The chambers' doorways** have his cave mouths' looks.

These are still in Minecraft's own pictures:
- the chambers' stone bricks (plain, cracked, mossy and chiseled, with their stairs and slabs);
- the gatehouses' polished andesite floors;
- the Unown walls' crying obsidian;
- the research centre's blue roof;
- the packed mud and coarse dirt patches.

These have no look of their own anywhere else on the map yet. I can give them one when John has seen them.

## Route 32's gate

The gate by the lake on Route 32 is the ruins' gate: its west side faces the ruins. Its name is now "Ruins of Alph
gate", and its windows say "Outside: ROUTE 32 one way, the RUINS OF ALPH the other." They used to say Violet City.

## In the Interior Viewer

- **Where to find it.** The "Johto: the Ruins of Alph" list now has three entries:
  - Route 36's gate;
  - the ruins themselves;
  - Route 32's gate.
- **The ruins.** They're shown as the game has them, every block with the game's pictures, the signs as the game's
  sign.
- **Looking round.** As with the ships: drag to turn, right-drag (or Shift and drag) to move across, and the wheel to
  come in and out.
- **Walking through.** Walk out of either gate's ruins side and you're taken to the ruins.
