# Retromon 0.14.0: smooth rounded UI, the battle screen and bag, slots, and the fixes from your test (18 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.14.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.13.0, with `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` beside it (unchanged). Compiled in CI; not run here.

## The whole UI, smooth and rounded
- **Shapes are drawn at your screen's own resolution**, with soft edges, not stepped in GUI pixels. That covers:
  - panels, pills and buttons;
  - type tags, the HP and EXP bars, the message bar and the arrows.

  The pixelated corners and stairs are gone everywhere.
- **One look throughout:** deep teal glass with a mint rim, as the battle plates have. That covers:
  - the START menu, POKéMON, the Pokédex and the Town Map;
  - CLOTHES, the save slots, the text box, the choice lists and the HUD.

  The old navy panels are gone.
- **The arrows** (▲▼◀▶ in the text) are drawn as smooth triangles.
- **BACK on the Pokédex:** a BACK pill on the status bar of every screen on the device (CLOSE on the START menu). Right click still
  works.

## The battle screen
- **Your plate sits low, beside FIGHT.** Its bottom is level with the bottom of the buttons; theirs is top right.
- **"What will CHARMANDER do?"** is on its own pill to the left of the buttons, clear of them.
- **The buttons:** the three round buttons no longer overlap, and RUN sits above BAG.
- **The move panel** (type, PHYSICAL or SPECIAL, how well it works, POWER, ACCURACY and BACK) is one rounded card above the
  moves.
- **The bag** is one card:
  - two tabs, POKé BALLS and MEDICINE, each saying how many kinds you have or "Empty";
  - your items as pills with their counts;
  - what the picked one does, and BACK.

  With no balls it says so: "No POKé BALLS yet. A POKé MART sells them."
- **After a win you celebrate,** a different way each time: arms up and hopping, the floss, a fist pump or a wave.
- **Trainers throw:** you and the trainer you're battling throw your Poké Balls overarm, arm back over the shoulder and through,
  not just a flick of the hand.

## The intro and the save slots
- **The opening is just INSID3 TH3 CUB3, presents.** No star, and no star over the O of the title.
- **Prof. Oak talks the whole time** he's speaking, at one steady pace. Clicking no longer changes it.
- **The slots are rounded cards,** the picture inset with rounded corners and the SLOT tag on a pill.
- **DELETE:** a DELETE pill on each used slot (or Delete or X on the picked one). It asks first. A deleted save is moved aside,
  not wiped.

## In the world
- **Oak stops you where you are.** Stepping into the tall grass (or the tree line) before you have a Pokémon, you're held just
  where you stood. You're no longer thrown back several blocks.
- **The old man stops you where you are too,** north of Viridian, the moment you step out. The camera turns to him only if
  he's beside you; otherwise it stays on you.
- **Your rival comes round Oak:** he turns up on the side of you away from Oak, so he no longer walks through him.
- **After your rival's battle he's still there.** He stays while you read the end of it, looks at you a moment, then walks off.
- **Your Pokémon comes out a step and a half behind you,** not inside you. It keeps that distance when you stand still.
- **CLOTHES:**
  - picking a CAP STYLE or BACKWARDS now puts the cap on (before, they did nothing unless HAT was CAP);
  - B (or Esc) keeps what you chose, as the newer games' wardrobes do;
  - UNDO puts back what you came in with.
