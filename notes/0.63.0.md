## The game's side
- Which hall: the room you're in, by the start of its name (every floor of SILPH CO., every cabin of the S.S. ANNE).
  Outside, a place that belongs to one: the S.S. ANNE's bow.
- **Light in the game:** each hall's planks, walls and rock are laid from where each block stands (as the map's looks
  are). So a block of wall is one block model wherever it is. The thirty halls add about 5,000 block models (about
  12% more than 0.62.0) instead of over 25,000.
- A hall goes up on whole repeats of its pictures, a few blocks from right over you, so its planks and walls fall
  exactly as the viewer shows them.
