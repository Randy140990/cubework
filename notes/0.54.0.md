## CELADON CITY: the GAME CORNER and the DEPT. STORE, inside (12:19)
- **You can go in now:**
  - the GAME CORNER through the middle of its glass front, under the porch;
  - the DEPT. STORE through either of its two glass doors. You come out of the door you went in by.
- **Every floor is FireRed's own map,** a tile a block.

### The DEPT. STORE
- **Its floors:**
  - a lilac hall at the back of every floor, with the stairs and the elevator;
  - the shop floor in warm orange tiles;
  - white counters with a green trim.
- **1F:** the SERVICE COUNTER (the receptionist inside its ring, on yellow carpet), sofas, the store's terminals and the layout board.
- **2F, the TRAINER'S MARKET:**
  - racks of goods and glass cases;
  - two clerks, as the first games: one sells GREAT BALLs, SUPER POTIONs, REVIVEs and the rest; the other sells nine TMs (DOUBLE TEAM, REFLECT, RAZOR WIND, HORN DRILL, EGG BOMB, MEGA PUNCH, MEGA KICK, TAKE DOWN, SUBMISSION).
- **3F, the TV GAME SHOP:**
  - the counters with their TVs and SUPER NES (look at them);
  - the posters;
  - the clerk teaches **COUNTER**, once (FireRed's tutor).
- **4F, WISEMAN GIFTS:** the POKé DOLL and the evolution stones, as before.
- **5F, the DRUG STORE:** shelves of bottles. Two clerks: the X items (X ACCURACY, GUARD SPEC., DIRE HIT, X ATTACK and the rest), and the vitamins (HP UP, PROTEIN, IRON, CARBOS, CALCIUM).
- **The ROOFTOP SQUARE:** up the stairs from 5F.
  - Its green railing, the table and chairs on a lilac rug.
  - **The vending machines:** FRESH WATER ¥200, SODA POP ¥300, LEMONADE ¥350.
  - **The thirsty girl:** give her a drink and she gives you a TM for it, once each (FRESH WATER, ICE BEAM; SODA POP, ROCK SLIDE; LEMONADE, TRI ATTACK). She and the man by her no longer hand the TMs out just for talking.
  - A drink is also what gets you past Saffron's thirsty guards.
- **The elevator:** its orange doors on 1F to 5F; inside, the buttons ask "Which floor?" (5F to 1F).
- **The stairs** run sideways, by the hall's wall, as FireRed's, up or down to the next floor.

### The GAME CORNER
- **Its looks:**
  - its gold carpet and sky-blue walls;
  - the counter with its glass case;
  - the prize shelves on the back wall, and an emblem of our own (a gold coin with a red seven);
  - the violet stage at the back, with a row of lit bulbs along its front, as the roof outside.
- **The slot machines:** five rows of them, each facing its blue stool, with the people sitting at them. Play one and it's FireRed's "A slot machine! Want to play?", then "A COIN CASE is required...". The broken ones say so.
- **TEAM ROCKET's grunt** by the poster, and the poster's switch ("Hey! A switch behind the poster!? Let's push it!"). The stairs down stay hidden for now (the ROCKET HIDEOUT comes next).
- **The PRIZE ROOM is built too** (its counter, clerks and prize shelves), but there's no door into it on your map yet. Where would you like it?
