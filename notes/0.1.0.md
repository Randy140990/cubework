# Route One 0.1.0 (working name): first build (17 Nov 2026)

Mod id `retromon`, jar `route-one-0.1.0+1.21.11.jar`. Fabric 1.21.11 + Fabric API, no Cobblemon needed.
Repo: /home/claude/route-one (compile checks on scratch, branch check/routeone-0.1, results on ci-check-routeone).
Compiled clean in CI; not run here (John tests).

## Setup
- Jar in mods, with Fabric API.
- Kanto world folder (or its zip) in `.minecraft/routeone`.
- Title screen: Route One (top left), Press Start, NEW GAME.
  - Copies the map to the save "Route One": cheats on, Adventure mode, the map maker's player removed, marker `routeone.json`.
  - Loads you at Pallet Town (-806, 18, 700), then Oak's welcome and the starter choice.
  - CONTINUE when the save exists.

## In it
- **Camera:** fixed 2.5D, pitch 55, FOV 34, never turns. North up, or diagonal on F8 or in OPTION. Scroll zooms 16 to 64.
- **Walking:** WASD in the camera's directions, no jumping, step-up of 1.1.
- **Keys:** Space is A (the jump key), Backspace/Esc is B, Enter is START. F7 tries the camera in any other world.
- **Wild battles:** Route 1 grass (short and tall grass, fern, large fern) at 25/256 a step; Pidgey and Rattata L2 to 5.
- **Trainer:** Youngster Joey at (-790, 600), facing south, sight 6. He shows "!", walks up, talks, battles (Rattata L4), pays prize money.
- **Gym leader:** Brock by Oak's lab (-798, 706). Geodude L12 and Onix L14; Boulder Badge.
- **Viridian:** the nurse (-785, 561) heals (YES/NO); the clerk (-766, 549) sells Poké Balls ¥200 and Potions ¥300.
- **Battles:** on the spot. Your Pokémon near left, theirs far right, using the most open of 8 directions. Wipe in.
  - Gen 2 boxes, FIGHT/PKMN/PACK/RUN. HP bars drain; lunge, flicker, faint, ball shakes; cries.
  - Note-block jingles for the title, heal, spotted, catch, level up, win and badge.
- **Engine:** Gen 2 rules (DVs, stat exp, damage, crits, type chart, accuracy, catch, EXP, escape, prize).
- **START menu:** POKéMON (with summary), BAG (Potion), CARD, SAVE, OPTION, EXIT.
- **Models:** the Cobblemon Bedrock models, built as Minecraft models with their idle animations (own Molang). Charmander's flame glows. Credit and CC BY-NC licence in the jar.
- **Commands:** `/routeone place|remove|heal|level|give|reset`.
