## What's wrong (my side)

The viewer lights each room live (its own lamps, window light, shadows); the game draws the same blocks with flat,
even light. So the textures match and the light doesn't. I said the viewer is the game, and for light it isn't.

## The plan

1. One light for both: work each room's light out once (daylight in through the windows and doors, the lamps, soft
   shadows and the dark in corners and under things), keep it with the room, and have the game and the viewer both
   draw it. The viewer's "In game" view draws only that, so what it shows is what the game shows.
2. Metal and glass: the same sheen everywhere (the Poké Center's blue beams, the Mart's, the glass tables), the glass
   tables all one design without the stepped white lines.
3. Oak's lab: nobody behind the NOTES board.
4. Route 2 gate: no bar across the way out at the top; the floor as clean as the viewer's.
5. Viridian: the gym's steps in the path's look; the item ball where you can reach it.
6. Then the farm and pet Retromon (13:27) in the Creature Viewer.
