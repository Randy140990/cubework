  - The map is **Retromon-Map-1.0.4.jar**, your 12:43 save (Footage_5).

## Map versions

- **Every save you send is a new map version.** This one is 1.0.4, the next is 1.0.5, and so on, and the mod is
  told which one it's made for.
- **An old map jar is refused.** With an older map jar in your mods folder, NEW GAME and CONTINUE both stop. The
  screen says which jar you have and which to put in ("Your mods folder has Retromon-Map-1.0.0.jar, an older map.
  This Retromon needs Retromon-Map-1.0.4.jar ...").
- **Each new game notes the map it's made with.**

## CONTINUE brings your game up to the new map

- When you CONTINUE a game made with an older map, the loading page says "Bringing your game up to map 1.0.4...". Your
  game gets the new map's land before it opens. Games from before this release noted no map, so they get it too.
  That's your game from map 1.0.0.
- **What comes from the new map:** the land, everything built on it, and what stands on it.
- **What stays yours:**
  - your team, bag, badges, money and story;
  - who you've beaten and what you've been given;
  - the rooms inside, and everything you've put down with the PLACER.
- **What's cleared, so it starts again from the new land:**
  - what the Town Map remembered of the old land;
  - the map's small fixes, such as the DARK CAVE's Route 46 way in, which are done again on the new land.
- **If the new land has something built where you were standing,** you're put on the ground beside it.
- **The same goes for everyone who plays.** When you release new regions, players CONTINUE and their games get the
  new land, with their progress kept. Nobody has to start again.
