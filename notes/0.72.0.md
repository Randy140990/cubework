## Putting it in

- **Two jars.** The mod is Retromon-0.72.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar. The map is still Retromon-Map-1.0.1.jar, unchanged.

## The PLACER (OPTION, under EVENT PICKER)

- **What's in it: 470 things from FireRed's own maps**, place by place in the order you reach them:
  - **202 trainers**, each with FireRed's team, words and sight. 147 of them are FireRed's own that the mod didn't
    have. The rest are the mod's own, matched to FireRed's by name or, in the caves, by kind.
  - **74 people:** the mod's own townsfolk outside past Viridian.
  - **75 item balls:** 65 of them are the mod's own, matched to FireRed's. The other 10 are Red and Blue's, which
    FireRed doesn't have, so you can place them or take them away.
  - **83 hidden items.**
  - **36 for STRENGTH:** 24 boulders, 4 floor switches and 8 rock barriers.
- **Each one shows FireRed's spot:** its step on FireRed's own map, the way it faces, how far a trainer sees, and its
  team or item.
- **PLACE HERE (A).** Stand where it goes, face the way it looks, and press A. It's there straight away, facing that
  way.
- **WALK AND PLACE (W)** for doing a whole route. You go back out on the map with a card at the top saying what
  you're placing:
  - **P** places it where you stand, and moves on to the next one in that place not placed yet;
  - **N** skips to the next;
  - **U** undoes the last one;
  - **X** stops.
- **TAKE AWAY (X)** for one of the mod's you don't want there. **PUT BACK (U)** undoes your placing of that one.
- **Saved as you go** to config/retromon_placed.tsv in your game folder (the screen shows the full path). Send me that
  file and I'll build your placings into the mod, so they're there for everyone.
- **Only for whoever runs the game**, as the pickers are.

## Trainers outside

- **Nobody stands outside past Viridian unless you've placed them**, as you asked at 18:19. Once placed, they stay
  exactly where you put them. They're not nudged to "good ground" the way the old guessed spots were.
- **They see as FireRed's do:** straight ahead, as many steps as FireRed gives them (two blocks a step on your map),
  and one step wide. Walk past beside or behind them and they don't see you. Face one across a path and you can't
  get by without battling.
- **Their battles** are FireRed's teams, prize money and words.

## STRENGTH boulders

- **A on a boulder:** "It's a big boulder, but a POKéMON may be able to push it aside." With one of yours that knows
  STRENGTH and the RAINBOWBADGE, you're asked "Would you like to use STRENGTH?"
- **YES:** walk into a boulder and it slides one step (two blocks) that way, if there's floor and nothing in the way.
- **On a floor switch:** it stays there for good, and that switch's rocks crumble away.
- **Leave the floor:** any boulder not on a switch is back where it started, as in FireRed.
- **You can't walk through a boulder or the rocks.**
- **VICTORY ROAD's rooms had boulders built into their blocks.** Those blocks are taken out as you go in, so the
  boulder you push is the only one there.
- **SEAFOAM's boulders aren't placed.** They work with holes and currents there. They're in the PLACER's list if you
  want them.
