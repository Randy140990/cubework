## Putting it in

- **Two jars.** The mod is Retromon-0.71.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar. The map is still Retromon-Map-1.0.1.jar, unchanged.
- **Take out the 0.70.0 mod jar.** Only one Retromon jar can be in the mods folder.
- **Your save carries on.** Everything here works in a save you already have.

## The event picker (OPTION, under PARTY PICKER)

- **Every step of the sheet.** All 87 Kanto steps are in a list, in story order. Each one shows where it is, what
  happens, and what you need and get.
- **BEFORE IT or IN IT.** BEFORE puts you just outside, or on the way to it. IN puts you into the room, or right at it.
- **The story as it would be then.** Everything before that step is done:
  - your badges, the key items and HMs, and the gifts and TMs;
  - the trainers and rivals that story says you've beaten;
  - the towns you can FLY to;
  - money and supplies for that point.
- **Your party.** You get a party at that point's levels, from your starter's line, as the PARTY PICKER makes it. P
  keeps your own party instead. S changes the starter. You can still use the PARTY PICKER afterwards, for level 70s
  at the ELITE FOUR, say.
- **Your own game is kept aside.** The first jump keeps it. MY OWN GAME (or O) brings it back, and puts you back where
  you were.
- **Only for whoever runs the game**, as the PARTY PICKER is.

## The story steps that weren't in

- **No PIKACHU starter.** Only the three. The PARTY PICKER has only the three as well.
- **JESSIE and JAMES.**
  - **Viridian:** they're standing by the sign as you come up from Route 1. They now appear any time until you've met
    them, not only before the POKéDEX, and they always have somewhere to stand. If they still don't show, send me
    `logs/latest.log`: it says where they were put, or that they couldn't be.
  - **Where Let's Go has them:** they battle you again at MT. MOON (B2F), on the ROCKET HIDEOUT's B4F before
    GIOVANNI, on the POKéMON TOWER's 7F, on SILPH CO.'s 11F, and on ROUTE 17 (the CYCLING ROAD) once you have seven
    badges.
  - **Their teams:** EKANS and KOFFING at MT. MOON, then ARBOK and WEEZING.
- **Your rival, three more times.** Each time he has FireRed's team for him.
  - **At the foot of NUGGET BRIDGE:** he comes down off the bridge as you reach it.
  - **On SILPH CO.'s 7F:** he's waiting by the warp pads.
  - **On ROUTE 22, with all eight badges:** he comes along the top path, as the first time.
- **The old man in Viridian.** Once he's had his coffee, he gives his catching lesson and then the TEACHY TV. Watch it
  from your BAG.
- **The Viridian GYM** is locked until you have the other seven badges. You're turned back at the door.
- **BILL.** The CLEFAIRY in his cottage asks for your help. Run the CELL SEPARATOR on his PC and BILL is himself
  again. Then he gives you the S.S. TICKET. His PC works as a normal PC after that.
- **The S.S. ANNE sets sail** once you're off her with HM01. The sailor won't let you back up after that. The ship
  stays on your map, as it's your build.
- **FLASH in ROCK TUNNEL.** It's pitch dark except for a small circle around you. If one of yours knows FLASH and you
  have the BOULDERBADGE, you're asked as you go in, and it lights the tunnel.
- **The TEA** comes from the old lady in the Celadon MANSION, as in FireRed. Any drink from the Dept. Store roof still
  gets you past Saffron's guards too.
- **The GAME CORNER.**
  - **The COIN CASE:** the man in the Celadon DINER gives it to you.
  - **Coins:** the clerk sells 50 for ¥1000.
  - **The slot machines:** they work, with symbols of our own.
    - Bet 1 to 3 coins.
    - A spins, then A stops each reel.
    - Three in a line pay out: SEVENs 300, BARs 100, BELLs, STARs and MOONs 15, CHERRIES 8.
  - **The prize corner (Red and Blue's prizes):**
    - **POKéMON:** ABRA, CLEFAIRY, NIDORINA, DRATINI, SCYTHER and PORYGON.
    - **TMs:** TM23, TM15 and TM50.
    - **The third counter:** FireRed's held items.
  - **The COIN CASE from your BAG** shows your coins.
- **MR. FUJI.** On the TOWER's 7F, beat the ROCKETs and talk to him. He takes you home, and gives you the POKé FLUTE
  there. Before, the boy in his house gave the flute away.
- **The NUGGET BRIDGE ROCKET** gives the five's NUGGET and asks you to join TEAM ROCKET, as in FireRed. That's ready
  for when he's standing there (see below).
- **The AIDEs.** The ITEMFINDER needs 30 kinds caught, and the EXP.SHARE needs 50.
- **The Cinnabar GYM's door** needs the SECRET KEY. The first time, you use it and the door opens.
- **The SECRET KEY.** Picking it up on the MANSION's B1F didn't put it in your BAG before. Now it does.
- **CERULEAN CAVE** is for the CHAMPION only. Until then, you're turned back at its mouth.
- **CUT trees you place.** Any little tree made as Route 2's two are (a dark oak fence trunk with cherry leaves) can be
  CUT, wherever you put it. Nothing needs adding on my side.
- **Speed up to x8.** TAB goes 1x, 2x, 3x and on up to 8x, then round again.
