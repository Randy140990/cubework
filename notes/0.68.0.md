# Retromon 0.68.0: a pass across all of Johto's outside (John's 26 Nov 09:20)

"I'd like you to have a pass across all of Johto and touch up any external materials."

## How the pass was done

I wrote a new tool, `tools/lookview.py`. It draws any part of the map as the game draws it with our looks:
- Minecraft's own pictures, or the look pack's in their place;
- then every look of ours, the smallest area's winning as in the game;
- seen from two corners, so all four sides are checked.

With `HILITE=1` it lists every face of every built block that's still in Minecraft's own picture. The ground, trees,
plants and water are left out of that list.

I ran it over the whole of Johto, every town and every route, tile by tile. I also ran a second sweep for small
rocks with no rock look on them.

**What it found.** Johto's towns and routes were nearly all done already. The one big gap was the cliffside west of
Cianwood. Johto's own looks had been held back there while you were building it. The rest were small bits.

## What's changed

- **The cliffside west of Cianwood (the way to the SAFARI ZONE's gate).**
  - It now has everything the rest of Johto has: its white, its grey, its shelf trims, its timbers in the cliff.
  - **Its paths:** the light grey stone along the cliff tops is now Kanto's soft, seamless path in that grey, laid
    four blocks across (`cliff_path_grey`).
  - **Its posts:** Cianwood's dark posts.
  - **Its two plank bridges:** they were built after the bridges' scan. They now have a bridge's look, the same as
    the third bridge there.
  - **Its signboard on the cliff:** the frame is pale wood, the pattern clean steel, the violet glass glows violet,
    and the lamps are lit.
- **Ecruteak.**
  - The top of the TIN TOWER's spire (its gold and dark metal, above where the tower's looks stopped) now matches the
    rest of the spire.
  - The house by the BURNED TOWER had two whole roof blocks that its roof's look had missed. They're done now.
  - Its cyan mat out front is a clean woven mat.
- **Olivine.**
  - The lighthouse's pale stairs up the lantern room are white like its walls, and its dark studs are bolts.
  - The gate at the west end of the harbour fence is white like the fence.
  - The pale shutters by the harbour look like Pewter's.
- **The NATIONAL PARK.** The last bits of the swan are in its marble, and the gate's little plaques are in the park's
  stone.
- **Mahogany.** The plaza paving's edges (where the ground steps) now match its top. The pale shutters by the west
  gate look like Pewter's.
- **Goldenrod.** The carved stone bricks over a doorway now match the trim.
- **By the water.** Two little spruce benches (one on the Whirl Islands' shore, one west of Olivine) have a bench's
  slats.
- **Rocks.**
  - Two boulders by gates had been missed by the rock scan, because a rock look is never laid over a gate's
    footprint: one by Olivine and one by the Lake of Rage. They're boulders now.
  - One cobble block in a Lake of Rage rock is done.
  - The two tuff blocks in Cianwood's cliff are now the cliff's rock.

The sweep after the changes finds nothing built in Johto still in Minecraft's own pictures. The only thing left is a
few sea pickles under the Lake of Rage, which are plants.
