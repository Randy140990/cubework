## The DARK CAVE and the ICE PATH (your two zips)

Both are rooms of their own, as the other caves are. They're built from the floors in your zips, and joined up the
way HeartGold's warps join them.

### DARK CAVE

- **The Violet side.** In from **ROUTE 31** (your mouth by Violet, at the top of the cave's west corridor) and from
  **ROUTE 46** (its bottom right).
- **The Blackthorn side.** You get there through the doorway past the big pool. You swim the pool, as SURF would.
- **Out at the top right onto ROUTE 45**, by your mouth south of Blackthorn.
- **It's pitch dark** until one of your team uses **FLASH**, as ROCK TUNNEL is. FLASH needs the ZEPHYRBADGE or the
  BOULDERBADGE.
- **HeartGold's items:** POTION, HYPER POTION, FULL HEAL, DIRE HIT and REVIVE.
- **The BLACKGLASSES man** on the Blackthorn side, with HeartGold's words. He gives you his BLACKGLASSES.
- **Walled up:** the two doorways at the tops of the Violet side's east corridors. The map ran off the screen there, and
  HeartGold has no way out there.

### ICE PATH

- **1F.** In from **ROUTE 44**, out to **BLACKTHORN CITY**.
- **The floors.** Ladders go down to B1F. From there you go on to B2F (the Mahogany side), B3F, then B2F's Blackthorn
  side, back up to B1F's lower part, and up to 1F's way out to Blackthorn. This is HeartGold's order.
- **The ice.** The blue ice is the slippery ice. Step on it and you slide that way until something stops you (a wall, a
  crystal, a boulder) or you slide off onto the floor. Stopped on the ice, walk any way and you slide that way.
- **B1F's boulder puzzle, as HeartGold's.**
  - Four STRENGTH boulders and four holes. Fall through a hole and you land on B2F's ice.
  - Push a boulder into a hole and you see "The boulder fell through!". It then lies on B2F's ice under that hole: "It
    has sunk in and cannot be moved."
  - **You need all four.** Without the fallen boulders there's nothing to stop on, and B2F's ice can't be crossed to its
    ladder down (I checked every slide).
  - **Where the boulders start:** each one push from its hole. From HeartGold's own starting spots, the crystal maze as
    it was traced only let one boulder reach a hole.
  - STRENGTH needs the RAINBOWBADGE or the PLAINBADGE.
- **B3F's ROCK SMASH rock** stands in front of the NEVERMELTICE.
  - "It's a rugged rock, but a POKéMON may be able to smash it."
  - With one of your team that knows ROCK SMASH, and the ZEPHYRBADGE or the BOULDERBADGE, it breaks apart.
  - It's back the next time you come onto that floor, as in the games.
- **HeartGold's items:** HM07 WATERFALL out on 1F's ice, PROTEIN, PP UP, IRON, FULL HEAL, MAX POTION and the
  NEVERMELTICE.
- **The man on 1F** who dropped the HM: "Walking on ice makes your feet freeze!"
- **Their looks.**
  - The Ice Path has the Seafoam Islands' ice and snow, a frosted floor and glassy pale slide ice.
  - The Dark Cave has a slate floor, brown-grey cliffs and Mt. Silver's stone steps.
- **Left out:**
  - The Dark Cave's ROCK SMASH rocks and its STRENGTH boulder. The trace didn't have them, and put down by guesswork
    they could shut a way.
  - Two items, TM54 (FALSE SWIPE) and the BLACK FLUTE, which aren't in the mod.
  - B2F's TM72 (AVALANCHE).

## The MAGNET TRAIN

- **The OFFICER names both cities:** "We'll soon depart SAFFRON for GOLDENROD. Are you coming aboard?"
- **You're led on board.** After "Right this way, please." he steps aside from the gate. You walk through it, across the
  platform, and in at the train's middle door.
- **The ride you asked for at 12:31.**
  - The train pulls out on your line and into the tunnel.
  - Then the ride: the train side on, crossing from left to right along a stretch of your own Goldenrod viaduct (its
    quartz piers, copied from your save). Rows of our trees run either side.
  - The camera waits for it at the left, goes along with it through the middle with the trees rushing past, and lets
    it go off the right.
  - It then comes out of the other city's tunnel and pulls in under the station's roof.
- **Getting off.**
  - In the station you step off at the train's door and walk out through the gate. The OFFICER stands out of the way.
  - Once you're past the barrier, he's back at his gate, turned to you: "We have arrived in GOLDENROD. We hope to see you
    again."
- **The stations' track ends are open.** Goldenrod's line runs out through its right-hand wall, Saffron's through its
  left. The opening is framed in each station's trim, with the rails running on into the dark.
- **The rail PASS:** still not asked for. HeartGold's comes from the COPYCAT in Saffron once the power's back on. Say
  when you want it.
- **Not done:** the train's doors opening and closing. The train is one model, and its doors are drawn open.
