# Retromon 0.3.0: the start, the stages and the new look (17 Nov 2026)

Two jars now: `retromon-0.3.0+1.21.11.jar` (the mod, ~19 MB) and `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` (the map,
~158 MB, rarely changes). Both go in mods, with Fabric API (Iris + Sodium optional, for Tempered Light).
Repo: /home/claude/route-one, compile checks on scratch (check/retromon-0.2 → ci-check-retromon). Not run here.

## Start
- Title → CONTINUE (every Retromon save slot, newest first, with the trainer's name and when last played) or
  NEW GAME.
- NEW GAME: Oak's welcome (Yellow's lines), boy or girl, your name (presets or type one), your rival's name,
  "Your very own POKéMON legend is about to unfold!", then the map is copied from the map jar to a new slot
  (Retromon, Retromon 2, ...) and you load into Pallet Town. No map picker; `.minecraft/retromon` still works
  for map makers. Without the map jar, NEW GAME says so.
- You look like the hero you picked (boy or girl skin) in a Retromon save.

## Battles
- Set-piece stages built over where you are (your screen only), taken down after: route field with trees and
  tall grass, forest clearing, cave rock bowl, water's edge, gym floor with pillars, town square. Picked by place.
- Always midday and clear on the stage. Three white flashes and closing bars on the way in; the intro music.
- Balls: yours and trainers' Pokémon are sent out of a ball (arc, poof, cry); throws, shakes, stars on a catch.
- Moves: particles by type (a stream for special moves, a burst for physical, sparkles for stat moves) and the
  games' own sound effects; hits shake the screen (harder when super effective); faints sink away.
- First catch of a species: "New POKéDEX data will be added", then its Pokédex entry (Gold's), then nickname.
- EXP bar fixed (it started at zero). Esc opens Minecraft's menu and comes back to the battle.

## Look and sound
- Poppins font (OFL) across the UI, cream highlights, Let's Go-style battle layout: foe card top right, yours
  bottom left with EXP, commands stacked right (FIGHT, POKéMON, BAG, RUN), moves with type tabs and PP, and the
  picked move's type, category, power and accuracy.
- Talking to people: black cinema bars and the camera closes in on the two of you.
- The A press click on every text and choice; 33 of Yellow's sound effects rendered from its code.
- New nickname entry (no Minecraft text box). Music has its own volume (OPTION → MUSIC).
- Brighter, sunnier greens on grass and leaves in Retromon saves.

## World
- Nothing can be broken in a Retromon save; you're kept in Adventure mode (stops Axiom). `/retromon build on|off`.
- Encounters: one "step" per 4 blocks (the map's scale) and 4 steps of peace after each battle.
- Speed key R: 1x to 4x.
- People, trainers and item balls placed from the games' own coordinates over each marked place
  (`/retromon populate <place> [redo]`, also on `/retromon zone set`), on dry open ground, never in water, on a
  fence or a roof. Default places: Pallet Town, Route 1, Viridian City, Route 22, Route 2.
- Item balls: talk to them for the item (TMs show as found but can't be taught yet).

## Known gaps
- Placement is only as close as the place's box: mark each place's box to its edges for the best fit.
- The stage is built at least 26 blocks above the ground around you, so very tall builds nearby can poke in.
- Johto music, interiors, TMs/HMs, Town Map and the full Pokédex are next.
