# Retromon 0.4.0: Town Map, Pokédex, TMs and HMs, clothes, the look pack (18 Nov 2026)

`retromon-0.4.0+1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.3.0. The Kanto map jar (`retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar`) is unchanged.
Saves from 0.3.0 carry on. Compiled in CI; not run here.

## START menu
POKéDEX, POKéMON, BAG, MAP, CLOTHES, CARD, SAVE, OPTION, EXIT.

## Pokédex
- KANTO (151) and NATIONAL (251) lists (Tab or click). Seen ones show their portrait; owned ones a Poké Ball.
- The page: number, name, and for owned ones the kind, types, height, weight and Gold's entry.
- WHERE TO FIND IT for any seen Pokémon (its wild tables and one-off spots), and ON MAP to see them marked.

## Town Map
- Kanto and Johto (Tab), laid out from the Pokégear's landmarks (Crystal), joined by the games' route connections.
- You are here (red pulse); places you've been bright, the rest dimmed; arrows or the mouse to pick a place.
- FLY HERE for a town you've been to, if a Pokémon of yours knows FLY and you have the THUNDERBADGE.
  `/retromon badge thunder` (or `all`) for testing. You land in the middle of the town's marked area.

## Portraits
- Every Pokémon drawn from its own model (three-quarter view, idle pose) as a 128 px picture:
  `tools/portraits.py` → `textures/gui/mon/`. Used on party cards (menu and battle) and in the Pokédex.

## TMs and HMs
- One item per move, numbered as in Gold/Silver (or Yellow for Gen 1-only TMs), in a TM & HM pocket.
- Use on a Pokémon from the bag: "Booted up a TM!", compatibility from Gold/Silver plus Yellow for the 151,
  forget-a-move if it has four. TMs are used up, HMs aren't.
- Where they come from: Kanto's TM item balls, each gym leader's TM after the badge (Brock's BIDE ...
  Giovanni's FISSURE), people indoors with TM/HM gifts (HM houses, once interiors exist), Celadon's TMs.
- HMs can't be used on the map yet (Cut, Surf, Strength, Flash); FLY works from the Town Map.

## Clothes
- CLOTHES: skin, hair (6 styles) and colour, hat (cap, beanie, sun hat or none) and colour, top (jacket,
  tee, hoodie, vest) and colour, bottoms (jeans, shorts, skirt) and colour, shoes colour. 14 colours.
- Live preview of you (follows the mouse); DONE saves it in your save, CANCEL puts back what you had.
- The skin is built from layers (`tools/outfits.py` → `textures/outfit/`) tinted and stacked in game.

## Retromon Look pack
- Built in, off by default: Options → Resource Packs → Retromon Look. Pokémon-style grass top and side,
  tall grass, leaves, poppies, dandelions, bluets, dirt paths and sand (`tools/lookpack.py`). A first pass to
  shape together.
