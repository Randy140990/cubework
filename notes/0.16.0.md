## As detailed as Cobblemon's
Each model is built to the same scale of detail as the Cobblemon model it stands in for:

| Measure | Retromon | Cobblemon |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cubes, first stages | 33 to 61 | 34 to 68 |
| Cubes, second stages | 44 to 81 | 45 to 74 |
| Cubes, final stages | 69 to 115 | 88 to 155 |
| Painted texels | more for 11 of the 14; the other 3 (Tsunapir, Quailet, Plumquail) within 12% | |
| Animations | 5 to 7 | 1 to 7 |

- **Painting:**
  - rounded bodies, three boxes to a part so the edges are cut back;
  - an edge on each face, light from above, shading down each side.
- **Eyes:**
  - a highlight, a pupil, and the iris lit underneath;
  - a blink;
  - on the sides of the head for the goats, tapirs, quails and armadillos.
- **Wings:**
  - Pyribex and the quails have feathered wings: rows of separate feathers that fold at their sides and spread to fly;
  - the train and tail fans are separate feathers too.
- **Glow:** the ember goats' horns, flames, hooves and lava glow and flicker.
- **Sizes:** each is about as big overall as the Cobblemon model, in height and length. The comparison viewer gives the
  exact heights, lengths and widths in blocks.
- **Animations:**
  - standing, a battle stance, walking, calling out and getting hit, for every one;
  - flying and hovering for Pyribex and the quails.
- **Each family moves its own way:**
  - the armadillos tuck in their heads when hit;
  - the goats rear and bleat;
  - the tapirs trumpet with their trunks up;
  - the quails peck, bob their heads as they walk, and spread their wings to call;
  - the jerboas hop;
  - Grovadon stamps.

## The comparison viewer
A page with each creature beside its Cobblemon counterpart in 3D, animated as the mod animates them. It shows:
- each one's stats and evolution;
- their sizes in blocks;
- their model detail;
- a 1.8-block player for scale.

It's built by `tools/monviewer.py`.

## Tools
- **`tools/monlib.py`:** the workshop. It has:
  - cubes, rounded blocks and eyes;
  - painting in 3D;
  - texture packing;
  - Molang helpers.
- **`tools/monanims.py`:** the animation sets, for four legs, birds and hoppers.
- **`tools/mons.py`:** the fourteen creatures.
- **`tools/monshow.py`:** their screen pictures, the line-ups and the stat sheet.

## Not in this one
- Their own Pokédex entries, cries and names in speech. The entries still describe the species, for after testing.
