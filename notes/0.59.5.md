## Johto's music: Crystal's songs
Wherever Johto still played Kanto's songs, it plays Crystal's now:
- **Battles:** wild POKéMON, trainers, Gym Leaders, the rival, Team Rocket, the Champion, and the legendary beasts.
- **After you win:** the victory songs for trainers, wild POKéMON and Gym Leaders.
- **When a trainer spots you:** youngsters, lasses, Rockets, the rival, hikers, beauties, sages, POKéMANIACS, officers, kimono girls and Eusine.
- **Buildings:** the POKéMON CENTER, the Gyms, ELM's lab and the Game Corner.
- **Jingles:** healing, catching, riding the bike and surfing.

Kanto keeps its own songs. The Magnet Train's, the S.S. AQUA's, the Dance Theater's and Johto's evolution song are ready for when those are in.
