# Retromon 0.10.0: Let's Go battles (18 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.10.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.9.0, with `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` beside it (unchanged). Compiled in CI; not run here.

## The battle screen, in Let's Go's style
- **Plates:** dark see-through teal with a bright mint edge. The name, then the level ("Lv." and the number) and the Pokémon's
  gender on the right (♂ in blue, ♀ in pink, bigger than before). A thick HP bar with a white rim, green to yellow to red. Yours
  has the HP numbers under the bar and a thin EXP line beside them. Theirs is top right, yours bottom left.
- **Party balls:** six Poké Balls above each plate: one per Pokémon, grey for one that's fainted, empty rings for empty slots.
  A wild Pokémon has none (it's on its own).
- **Commands:** big round buttons along the bottom right (FIGHT pink, POKéMON green, BAG yellow), rising a little to the right,
  RUN smaller and blue above them (greyed in a trainer battle). The picked one grows and pulses, with a white pointer bobbing
  beside it. "What will PIKACHU do?" sits in a pill above them. Arrow keys go FIGHT, POKéMON, BAG, RUN; the mouse works too.
- The text box matches the plates (mint edge, dark teal).
- Pictures made by `tools/battle_ui.py`, drawn smooth.

## The field
- Let's Go's route battles: open grass in the middle, long grass and low hedges round its edge, then a deep wall of round
  trees all the way round (three rings), so there's no sky or edge showing through.
- Forest battles get the same wall, mixed trees; towns and the water's edge get an outer ring of trees too.

## The camera
- Further back and to the left of you, like Let's Go: you're in view at the left, your Pokémon in front of you, theirs across
  the field, their trainer at the far right.
- **Opening sweep:** it starts low on their side looking at them (and their trainer), then swings round to behind you.
- **Attacks:** it goes right in on the one attacking (much closer than before), then back.
