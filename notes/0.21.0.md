# Retromon 0.21.0: every move's effect, a Moves page, leafier armadillos (19 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.21.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.20.0. `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` goes beside it, unchanged. Compiled in CI; not run here. This pass is for reviewing everything in the creature viewer first. Battles in the game still play only the call and the hit.

## Move effects: all 251, one each
- **One effect per move:** each move's effect is its own model and animation (`assets/retromon/moves/<move>/`). It's made once, and any creature that uses the move plays it from its own mouth, back or body. A creature's model now keeps only its own motions: the wind-up, the lunge, the trunk aiming.
- **Anchors:** every effect has points for the user's back, body and the ground under it, and the target. Each creature's model records where its mouth, back and body are, so the same effect fits a Zipsy or a Grovadon.
- **Evolution stages:** every effect has three versions, and moves grow as creatures evolve. Razor Leaf fires 3 leaves at stage 1, 7 at stage 2 and 10 at stage 3; Ember throws 2, 3, then 4 flames; Poison Sting fires 5, 8, then 10 barbs. Each creature knows its own stage: first of a line 1, middle 2, last 3.
- **Type auras:** special moves wrap the user in a glow of its type while it attacks. Fire and Dragon get flames, Electric crackling bolts, Ghost, Dark, Poison and Psychic wisps, and the rest twinkling sparks.
- **Highlights:**
  - **Flamethrower:** a constant stream of flame, held for about 2 seconds and sweeping left to right. The user holds its pose while it pours out.
  - **Hydro Pump:** a much wider torrent with a jet inside it and splashes at the target, held for 1.5 seconds. Water Gun is a narrow jet.
  - **Thundershock and Thunderbolt:** forked bolts from mouth to target, with 2 or 3 bolts at higher stages.
  - **Thunder:** a bolt straight down from the sky.
- **Families of looks:**
  - **Blows:** punches, kicks, claws and slashes, fangs, pincers, drills and coils.
  - **Projectiles:** flames, leaves, needles, rocks, bones, coins, stars and bubbles.
  - **Streams and beams.**
  - **Clouds:** powders and gas.
  - **Sounds:** rings and notes.
  - **At the target:** shield bubbles, walls of light, weather across the field, and things happening to it (vortexes, cracks, spikes, webs).
  - **On the user:** stat arrows, healing sparkles and Zs.
- **First pass:** some are rougher than others. Flag any that need redoing.

## Viewer
- **Moves page:**
  - A ghost Retromon stands on the left and uses each move on a ghost target on the right, which flinches as damaging moves land.
  - Moves are listed by type, with Previous, Next, Replay and **Play all in turn**.
  - **Performed by** swaps the ghost for any of the 27 creatures, at that creature's own stage. With the ghost, pick stage 1, 2 or 3.
  - The panel gives each move's type, category, power, accuracy, PP, what it does, its TM or HM, and which of our creatures learn it by level.
- **Creature page:** one pick list ("Charmander · Cinderkid") replaces the family pills. Picking a creature shows its evolution line as the cards underneath. Its attack and move buttons play the moves' own effects.

## Models
- **Fernadillo and Brambadillo:**
  - Flat leaf blades lie over the bands like shingles, with leaves hanging down the sides.
  - A crossed sprig of leaves replaces the blocks on top.
  - The cheeks have become flat whiskers.
  - Brambadillo's thorns are violet-tipped leaves.
- **Grovadon:** six big broad leaves fan out over the grove, the sides hang with a shaggy mammoth coat of flat strands, and the beard is flat strands too.
- **Pyribex:** bigger wings whose feathers reach well down its sides, and a tail about three times longer in three swaying joints with the flame at its tip.
- **Tapirs:** spiked dorsal sails with pale ribs (3, 5 and 7 spines up the line); Tsunapir's head crest is spiked too.
- **Flight:** quails and shrikes keep their wings neatly folded while walking and open them out nearly twice as big to fly. Pyribex's open half as big again.
- **Claws:** Scrapkit, Ragemong and the porcupines have sharp hooked talons; Ragemong's are longest.
- **Galeshrike:** bigger, standing 2.7 blocks instead of 2.35.

## Tools
- **`tools/monmoves.py`:** the move effect library (`python3 tools/monmoves.py [moves]`).
- **`tools/movetext.py`:** what each move does, in plain words.
- **`tools/monlib.py`:** takes a creature's old built-in move effects out, and records its anchors and stage.
