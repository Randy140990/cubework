# Retromon 0.67.0: a quicker start, the farm creatures in the mod, and John's 16:28 walk round Kanto

## A quicker start (John's 16:07: "the mod is getting really slow to start")

**What was slow.** All the rooms' looks (37,644 of them) were states of a single block, `retromon:prop`. The time and
memory Minecraft takes to make a block's states grow with the square of how many it has. The CI measured it:

| States in one block | Time to make them | Memory they hold |
|---|---|---|
| 4,000 | 1.8 s | 62 MB |
| 8,000 | 5.1 s | 248 MB |
| 16,000 | 16.5 s | 991 MB |
| 37,644 (the old way) | about 90 s, by the same curve | about 5.5 GB |

That came on top of everything else at each start.

**What's changed.**
- **The looks are shared out over 160 blocks of 256 states each** (`retromon:prop`, then `retromon:prop_1` up to
  `prop_159`). In the CI, all the looks made this way (148 blocks of 256, 37,888 states) took 0.8 s and 49 MB.
- **The models go straight to Minecraft**, one for each state. They used to come from a blockstates file, whose every
  variant Minecraft checks against every state.
- **The shaders (Retromon Light 1.2.10)** list every one of the blocks, so each room is still lit as the viewer
  lights it.
- **The rooms are built again once, in the new blocks,** the first time you load your world with 0.67.0. That takes a
  moment, once only.

If it's still slow to start, send me `logs/latest.log` from your Minecraft folder and I'll see what's left.

## The farm and pet creatures are in the mod (16:07)

- **What's in.** All 29 models are in the jar: the cattle, sheep, pigs, chickens and horses (young, she and he), plus
  the dog and the cat in all seven coats each.
- **Placing one.** `/retromon place farm <kind>` puts one where you stand and tells you its coordinates. Tab
  completes the kinds, for example `moobelle`, `heelhound_golden` or `barnpurr_calico`.
- **How they behave.** They aren't wild, can't be caught and don't battle. Press A at one and it makes its own
  animal's sound (Minecraft's cow, sheep, pig, chicken, horse, wolf or cat, higher for the young ones). Then you see
  what it's like, and a pet's coat.
- **Placements.** When you tell me where they go, I'll put them there for good.

## John's 16:28 walk round Kanto

- **Route 12, by the TM27 gate.** The rock in the water against the gate's east side now has the sea rocks' look.
  The map scan had missed it because half of it is inside the gate's own area.
- **Torchflowers.** They now use the torchflower from the same pack as the grass and ferns (Shards), with its three
  variants picked at random, so they vary. Their leaves are on Retromon's greens, as the other flowers' are.
- **The Power Plant's top.** The yellow band and the white cap on it are seamless: one clean colour each, with no
  block lines.
- **The strip by Nugget Bridge.** The packed mud along the water's edge, to the right of the bridge on the way to
  Bill's, now has the cliff's rock look, with the grass's tufts onto it.
- **Route 4's steps by the Center (towards Mt. Moon).** They're no longer boards. They're the look pack's cobblestone
  and mossy cobblestone, with the moss's soft edge onto the cobble, as on Route 1.
- **Pewter's Museum roof.** The corners of the top roof's brick rim are brick now, not solid purple. Every upright
  face of a corner piece gets the rows, running on from the pieces either side.
- **Victory Road's buildings.** Their tops (the band, the long bars on it and the caps at the ends) are dark gold
  with a shine. The brick below them is as it was.
- **Buttons on stones.** Every button on the map was checked against what it sits on. The four on the rocks by
  Victory Road were the only ones not yet matched, and they now take the rock's own look.
- **Cinnabar's LAB.** The three solar arrays now run down each side of the roof, left and right of the dark rails,
  instead of down the middle. That makes six in all, each side the mirror of the other.

## The S.S. ANNE from outside, for review

The Interior Viewer has a new place, **S.S. ANNE (outside)**, under "Ships (for review)". It is John's ship block for
block, with each kind of block in its own colour drawn in our looks:

- painted steel, clean and seamless;
- timber in boards;
- brushed metal;
- tinted glass;
- lamps lit.

The shapes are Minecraft's, and only what can be seen from outside is drawn.

To look round it:
- drag to turn round it;
- right-drag, two fingers, or Shift and drag to move across;
- the wheel or a pinch to come in and out;
- W A S D to move across.

**The game's ship is unchanged.** This is for review first.
