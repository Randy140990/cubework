# Retromon 0.53.0: SILPH CO., photo mode, John's 11:16 round, GOLDENROD CITY (2026-11-23)

The map jar is unchanged (Retromon-Map-1.0.0).

## SILPH CO., all eleven floors and its elevator, as FireRed's
- **Every floor is FireRed's own map,** a tile a block: the walls, desks and PCs, machines, sofas, plants, the lobby's two fountains, the boardroom's walnut table. They're drawn as the building's outside: white cladding panels, SILPH blue, pale blue-grey tiles, dark wall tops between the rooms.
- **In through the middle of its glass front,** between the two dark posts.
- **The stairs** between floors, in their alcoves, up and down.
- **The warp pads,** FireRed's own pairs. A pad's partner can be on this floor or another. The pad you land on stays quiet till you step off.
- **The elevator:**
  - its steel doors on every floor;
  - inside, the buttons ask "Which floor?" (11F to 6F, then 5F to 1F);
  - walk out and you're on the floor you picked, or back where you got in.
- **FireRed's card-key shutters:**
  - Without the CARD KEY (5F): "It needs a CARD KEY!"
  - With it, you're let through. The first time it's "Bingo! The CARD KEY opened the door!"
- **FireRed's trainers, people and item balls** where it has them. FireRed's **hidden items** are there too: look at the spot.
- **7F:** the worker gives you **LAPRAS**.
- **9F:** the woman lets you rest ("You look tired. You should take a quick nap."), and your POKéMON are healed.
- **11F:** once you've beaten GIOVANNI, the PRESIDENT gives you the **MASTER BALL**.
- (Your rival's battle on 7F comes later. There's no battle for him in the mod yet.)

## Photo mode (11:24, for you, to take out later)
- **F6** (or OPTIONS, PHOTO MODE) goes in and out. The camera leaves you, with every look of the map on, for high shots.
- **Controls:**
  - WASD or the arrows move it;
  - SPACE and CTRL take it up and down;
  - Q and E turn it round, and R and F tilt it;
  - the wheel takes it in and out, and Z and X narrow and widen the lens;
  - SHIFT goes faster, and H hides the words.
- **While it's on:** you stay where you are, and everything on screen but the world is put away. F2 takes the picture.
- The map is drawn as far round you as Minecraft's render distance reaches. Stand near what you want in the shot.

## Doors and people
- **No more wait at doors** ("a delay going into stuff"). Once you're through, the next door works half a second later; before, it held you for nearly three seconds more.
- **Trainers indoors stand where the games have them.** The FIGHTING DOJO's no longer walk about. The ones who look about turn on the spot.
- **The COPYCAT's hidden NUGGET and LT. SURGE's trash cans** now work in the game. The rooms' "look here" spots weren't reaching the mod.

## Route 22 and the cliffs

## Saffron and Celadon (11:16)
- **The oak stairs under the windows** are stone sills: cream in Saffron, white in Celadon.
- **The doors** are new glass doors in a pale metal frame, with a push bar and a kick plate.
- **In Celadon:**
  - the GAME CORNER's sign is glossy enamel, in its own colour;
  - the lamps are one seamless dark green post, with no chiselled blocks at the top and bottom;
  - the fountain is pale dressed stone;
  - the benches are proper benches: wooden slats on dark green iron ends;
  - the green trims are flat, not shelving;
  - the pond by the house is quartz all through (the dripstone in it too), with no blue and white tiles;
  - the bands of tiles are warm white;
  - the pavement runs right to its edge on the way to Route 16.

## GOLDENROD CITY (11:16)
- **Its buildings are Celadon's,** so they have Celadon's looks: the walls, roofs, windows, the GAME CORNER, the glass doors and sills, the steps, the trims. Kanto's looks are on everything else: the trees, glass, fences, cliffs and signs.
- **Its own looks:**
  - the streets are red-brown brick paving, the pavements golden tiles, the kerbs pale stone;
  - the lamps are its own: dark bronze posts, seamless, with gold caps and amber globes;
  - the fences are crisp white with a gold cap on each post, and the columns fluted white with gold rings;
  - the gold on the arch is polished, and the benches are Celadon's new ones.
- **Left as they are for now:** the RADIO TOWER and the MAGNET TRAIN station, which are their own.
