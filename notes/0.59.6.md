(Retromon-Map-1.0.0).

## The real reason the pillars, cliffs and mushrooms kept their old pictures
- **The bug was in the mod, not the map data.** A few rows of the looks table were written `hill_top@4|drip_rock@4`.
- **What the mod did with them:** it read that as the number "4|drip_rock@4". The error then threw away **every** look of that
  block, all over the map.
- **The blocks it hit:**
  - stripped spruce wood and log (since 0.59.3);
  - stripped jungle wood and brown mushroom blocks (since 0.50);
  - pink terracotta (Route 23).
- **What that broke:** Oak's lab pillars, the Viridian posts, the Route 12 and Route 16 house pillars and the Diglett's Cave
  mound. Their rows were right; none of them was ever drawn.
- **Why I kept saying they were done:** my checks traced the rows against the map, but never checked that the mod could read
  them.
- **Fixed:**
  - Both ways of writing it are read now.
  - A row that can't be made is left out on its own (not the block's other looks), and the log says which:
    `Retromon looks: a ... look (...) couldn't be made, left out`.
  - `tools/checklooks.py` checks every picture the table names exists (none missing).
- **Also kept from earlier:**
  - Our looks go on as the outermost model, so another mod's model can't sit over them.
  - The log line `Retromon looks: ours are drawn for all N block states with a look (0 looks left out)` confirms it in game.
- **Stripped logs and woods, mushroom blocks:** wherever a look gives them a picture, they're now drawn as our own whole block
  ("cube"). Nothing of their old picture can show, even under a resource pack's connected textures. That's 462 rows.

- **The earlier creature feedback, applied:**
  - **Wingspans:** the three great birds have the five-jointed wings (as the quails' and Pyribex's). They fold neat along
    the flanks and open out nearly twice as big to fly. AZURDRAKE's frills are great fanned wings.
  - **Joints:** jointed legs with knees and hocks, feathered thighs and raptor feet on the birds, hooked claws.
  - **Jaws and horns** are one unbroken curve, not pieces that come apart:
    - PITJAW's and JADEJAW's sickle jaws reach forward and hook in, spines on their inner edges;
    - LYREHORN's horns sweep out and up into a lyre.
- **Kept clear of the Pokémon's own look:**
  - **The genets:** real genets' spots and rings. No cream ruffs or plumes.
  - **The numbats:** a real numbat's rust-red with white bands. NUMBOLT changes to match.
  - **The paper birds:** red washi paper, white on the back. CREASECRANE changes to match.
  - **The galagos:** fawn and slate bushbabies with brush tails, not pink or lilac.
  - **OARWYRM:** silver, as oarfish are.
  - **AZURIBBON:** no bead on its brow.
- **Screen pictures:** the party, Pokédex and summary pictures existed for only the first 81 creatures. The other 170 (all of
  Johto's among them) would have shown a missing texture. Now every one of the 251 has its picture.
