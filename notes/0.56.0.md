## What went wrong in 0.54 (and is fixed at the root)
- **The cause:** 0.54 copied Kanto towns' looks onto whole Johto towns (Pallet's and Viridian's onto Cherrygrove, Cerulean's onto New Bark, Viridian's and Cerulean's onto Blackthorn). That copied each Kanto building's own looks too, so they landed on every block of the same kind in the Johto town:
  - a Mart's pillars went on the Center;
  - Viridian's red chimney brick went on Blackthorn's mud brick steps;
  - Cerulean's grey pillars went on Blackthorn's grey walls;
  - the Gyms' tan went on Blackthorn's Gym;
  - Oak's lab's pillars went on Route 26's cliffs.
- **Now:** only the looks a whole Kanto town has (its paths, its lamps, its roofs' colours) go on a Johto town. Each Johto building gets its own looks, below.
