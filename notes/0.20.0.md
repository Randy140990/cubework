# Retromon 0.20.0: sharper quills, clawed toes, unique attacks, new critical (19 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.20.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.19.0. `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` goes beside it, unchanged. Compiled in CI; not run here.

## Battle
- **Confused stars:** three crisp pixel-art stars (outlined gold, pale centre). They're the same size on every creature, and they sit just above the highest solid part (head, shell, horns), not counting flames.
- **Critical:** a jagged "pow" burst with ten spikes and a red rim, plus a white shock ring.
- **Attacks:** each family has its own attack, with its name on the viewer button.

  | Attack | What happens | Used by |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | TACKLE | A body slam with a shock ring. | Armadillos, tapirs, quails |
  | SCRATCH | A leg swipe with three claw marks. | Goats, mongooses, female porcupines |
  | HORN ATTACK | A head-down charge with a spark. | Male porcupines |
  | PECK | A beak jab with a spark. | Shrikes |
  | QUICK ATTACK | A dash with speed lines. | Jerboas, gliders |
- **Water Gun / Hydro Pump:** Surfapir's jet is a third bigger than Tidapir's.
- **Poison Sting:** a volley of ten barbs.
- **Thundershock / Thunderbolt:** a jagged forked bolt, with crackling arcs glowing round the whole creature as it charges.

## Models
- **Porcupines:** the quills are sharp tapered spikes, not rods. Longer claws. Quilltusk has a longer tail.
- **Birds:** thick knuckled toes with hooked talons and a hind toe on the quails and shrikes, not sticks.
- **Galeshrike:** taller legs.
- **Jerboas:** Jerbit is 30% bigger, Jerbolt a little bigger.
- **Gliders:** shaped, pointed ears with pink insides, not squares.

## Viewer
- **Recall:** the red beam pulls it into the ball. Then the ball is thrown in an arc, bounces, pops open with a white flash, and it comes out glowing white before settling.
- **Sizes:** hidden effects no longer count toward a creature's height.
