# Retromon 0.27.0: your 0.26.0 notes, and colour throughout (20 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.27.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.26.0. Keep `Retromon-Map-1.0.0.jar` beside it (unchanged). Compiled in CI; not run here.

Look at the viewers first: the UI Viewer and the Interior Viewer have every change below that you can see.

## Title
- The scenery fills the screen edge to edge. No gaps at the sides.
- A new RETROMON wordmark: round, bold letters instead of the cubes.
- PRESS START sits in the middle of its pill.

## New game
- **Professor scene:** Oak, the creatures and your rival now stand in front of or behind each other properly as they move.
- **The green line** under the PROF. OAK tab is gone.
- **Character maker:** 12 skin tones (new: ivory, peach, olive, espresso) and 4 more hair colours (blonde, auburn, silver, chestnut).
- **Oak** has fair skin and grey hair again.

## The lines on buttons
They're gone everywhere: the SPACE Talk pill, the menu, the battle buttons and the panels. Each rounded shape is now drawn in one piece, so Minecraft can't slip a gap between its parts.

## START menu (Let's Go's layout)
- **At the top:** your partner in a round badge, ringed in its type's colour, with its name. Before you have one, a Poké Ball.
- **Five big round buttons, each its own colour:** POKéDEX red, POKéMON green, BAG amber, MAP blue, SAVE purple. Each has its picture on a pale face.
- **Under them, five smaller pills:** CLOTHES, CARD, WAIT, OPTION, EXIT.
- **Moving:** left and right go along a row, up and down go between the rows.

## Colour throughout
- **Choosing your first:** each card is in its type's colours: leafy green, fiery orange, sea blue. The one you're on lifts, brighter, with a white rim.
- **The shop, the bag and the battle bag:** every item is a coloured bar with its picture in a white circle and a Poké Ball mark at the end. Balls are red, medicine green, TMs blue, key items amber, ropes and repels purple.
- **Summary and Pokédex pages:** the background is the creature's own colour (a water one's blue, a fire one's orange). Its name sits on a band of that colour. On STATS, the bars are in its colour too.
- **"Added to your POKéDEX!":** the page is in the creature's colour.
- **Emotes over heads** (the heart, the star, the "!" over a trainer): smooth white bubbles with a clean picture, instead of the blocky ones.

## Professor's lab
- **Blue** walks in through the door. After the battle he walks out through the exit, not off to the bookshelf.
- **"BLUE received a TIDAPIR"** no longer shows a key item card. That card is only for things you get.
- **The battle in the lab happens in the lab.** No more forest.
- **The aides:** the one at his desk sits on his chair at the PC. Three of the others walk about between the shelves. The one at the big machine stays at it.
- **The talk camera** eases round to whoever you talk to, and pans back afterwards. It no longer jitters or snaps.
- **The whiteboard** only offers SPACE Look when you're right in front of it.

## Battles
- **Win, or catch one:** the celebration holds. You see the EXP, and Continue (A) takes you out when you're ready.
- **Your plate** is level with the text box beside it, top and bottom.
- **A wild one that faints** lies where it fell, and doesn't vanish.
- **The bag in battle** is redone. The two pockets are big coloured cards, and the items are coloured bars. ESC goes back.
- **The time of day** now waits until you leave the battle screen. Before, the server's battle ended first, so the sky jumped forward when you pressed Continue.
- **Why 19 or 20 HP?** Each creature has a little of its own variation, so the same species at the same level can have 18 to 20 HP. Moves can also miss: each has an accuracy.

## Viridian City
- **A Pokémon Center and a Poké Mart inside:** the nurse behind her counter (healing), the clerk at his (buying and selling), and a couple of people in each.
- The nurse and clerk who stood on the roofs are taken away, once, in an old save.
- The Center has its own music.

## In the world
- **ESC goes back** on every screen, OPTION included.
- **Your partner walks** beside you rather than floating along.
- **TAB** speeds you up too, not just the world.
- **Pallet Town's girl** ("I'm raising POKéMON too!") is moved to the flower beds by the roses. In an old save she moves there once.

## Retromon Light 1.1.0 (OPTION > SHADERS)
- **TEMPERED is gone.** SHADERS is now RETROMON or OFF.
- **Lamps and torches** throw a much stronger warm light at night, and glow themselves.
- **Nights** are bluer and moonlit.
- **Grass, flowers and leaves** sway in the wind.
- **Water** looks like Tempered Light's: clear in the shallows, deeper blue further out, the sky reflected at a low angle, with ripples.

## To check
- **The menu:** press ENTER and move round both rows. Then look at the shop and bag colours, a summary (STATS too), and a new Pokédex entry after a catch.
- **The lab:** Blue's walk in and out, the seated aide, the battle in the lab, and Continue after the win.
- **Viridian:** go into the Center and the Mart. Heal, then buy something.
- **Lamps at night:** WAIT to night, and look at the lamps and the water.

## Not in this one
- The bedroom and downstairs closer to Let's Go's.
- LANGUAGE and OPTIONS on the title screen.
- Reskinned, rounder blocks: later, as you said.
