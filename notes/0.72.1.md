## The whirlpools

- **They don't flash any more.** They were drawn in among the sea's see-through faces, and which was drawn first kept
  changing as you moved. Now they're drawn with the solid blocks: each pixel of the picture is either there or not
  (its edge dotted out into the sea), so nothing changes as you look round.
- **They spin.** The same whirlpool picture, turning a whole turn every 3.2 seconds.
- **Rocks can't take one away.** Each was drawn by a rock beside it; now it's drawn by the sea bed right under its
  middle. So you can move or remove rocks round them.
- **The north-west island's is 26 blocks further west:** at x -2479, z 712, off the island's north-west corner, on
  your way round its left side from Cianwood. If you want it somewhere else, stand on the spot and tell me your x and
  z (F3).
- **ROUTE 27 has its two,** placed from HeartGold's map against yours:
  - x -1136, z 766: just south of the grassy strip, at the top of the line of rocks;
  - x -1205, z 786: in the line of rocks, west of the big sandbank.
- They're still just to look at. Nothing stops you surfing through them yet.
