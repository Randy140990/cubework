# Retromon 0.5.0: battle feel (18 Nov 2026)

`retromon-0.5.0+1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.4.1 (Kanto map jar unchanged). Compiled in CI; not run here.

- **Into battle (Let's Go):** streaks swoosh across left to right, the screen washes white, then the white
  fades away on the stage.
- **Catch ring:** choosing a ball now aims first: a white ring round the wild Pokémon and a coloured ring
  closing inside it. The colour is the real catch chance (Gold/Silver sums: catch rate, HP left, status, ball):
  green Easy (50%+), yellow Fair, orange Hard, red Very hard. A or click throws, B goes back to the bag.
  Timing doesn't change the odds (the originals' catch maths stays).
- **Attacks:** the camera swings behind the attacker to watch the move go across, then back. The hit flashes
  the move's type colour over the screen. Particle amounts scale with the move's power.
- **Big moves:** Thunderbolt, Thunder and Zap Cannon bring a real bolt of lightning down (flash and thunder);
  beam moves (Hyper Beam, Solar Beam, Ice Beam, Aurora Beam, Psybeam, BubbleBeam, DragonBreath) fire a beam;
  Earthquake, Magnitude and Fissure shake the ground; Flamethrower, Fire Blast, Fire Spin and Sacred Fire
  stream fire; Surf, Hydro Pump, Whirlpool spray water; Blizzard, Gust, Twister, Razor Wind, Whirlwind swirl;
  Explosion and Self-Destruct explode.
- **Already in (confirmed):** moves are coloured by type with PP left/max; a move with no PP can't be picked
  ("No PP left for this move!"); with none left at all your Pokémon uses STRUGGLE, as in the games.
