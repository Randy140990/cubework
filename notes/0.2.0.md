# Retromon 0.2.0: the big pass (17 Nov 2026)

Renamed from Route One. Mod id `retromon`, jar `retromon-0.2.0+1.21.11.jar`. Fabric 1.21.11 + Fabric API.
Iris optional (Tempered Light switches on with it). Repo: /home/claude/route-one (compile checks on scratch,
branch check/retromon-0.2, results on ci-check-retromon). Not run here (John tests).

## Setup
- Jar in mods, with Fabric API (and Iris + Sodium for the night lights).
- Title screen: Retromon button, Press Start, NEW GAME, then pick the Kanto map from your worlds (any save;
  zips or worlds in `.minecraft/retromon` also listed). It copies it to a save called "Retromon".
- The 0.1 "Route One" save doesn't carry over: start a NEW GAME.

## Controls
- WASD walk (screen up is north), Shift run, Space is A (talk, confirm), Backspace or right click is B.
- Enter is START (menu), R cycles game speed 1x/2x/3x (single player), mouse wheel zooms (24 to 40).
- Mouse works on every screen: click options, starters, moves, items.

## New
- **Camera:** north up only (F8 and diagonal gone), closer, no bounce. Battles get their own camera behind
  your Pokémon; plants around the battle are cleared while it runs and put back after.
- **Walking:** can't walk into water (you climb out if you're in it); no jumping; one-block steps only by stairs and slabs.
- **Battles:** Gen 2 engine with every move effect, items in battle, ball bonuses; the ball is thrown, shakes and
  pops; nickname after a catch; EXP bar; level-up, new move and evolution prompts.
- **UI:** new look across title, starter, dialogue, battle, menu (party cards, summary with stats and EXP,
  bag pockets, trainer card with badges, options with text speed), mart (buy and sell).
- **Music:** Yellow's soundtrack, rendered from the game's music code, per place, with battle, victory,
  gym and jingles. Johto places use the nearest Kanto tune for now.
- **Places:** banner on entering ("ROUTE 1"). Zones set with `/retromon zone` (Pallet Town, Route 1 and
  Viridian City set by default).
- **Wild Pokémon:** Yellow's Kanto tables per place (grass, caves, water, rods), Silver's Johto tables
  (morning, day, night). Gen 1 and Gen 2 slot odds.
- **People:** all 329 Kanto trainers (with their sight, lines and teams), 344 townsfolk, gym leaders with
  badges, Elite Four; each with a skin. 38 one-off Pokémon (Snorlax, legendaries, gifts, trades) to place.
- **Shaders:** Tempered Light 2.10.5 carried in the jar and switched on through Iris while you play; your
  own shader choice comes back when you leave.

## Commands
- `/retromon place nurse|clerk [mart]|trainer <key>|person <key>|pokemon <id>`, `/retromon remove`
- `/retromon zone corner1|corner2|set <place>|clear <place>|list|here`, `/retromon go <place>`
- `/retromon list trainers <place>|people <place>|pokemon`
- `/retromon level <n>|give <species> <level>|item <item> <count>|money <n>|heal|reset`

## Known gaps

- Interiors (one Mart and one Pokémon Centre as schematics) are the next phase.
