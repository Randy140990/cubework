# Retromon 0.18.0: shaped eyes, goat noses and teeth, cloven hooves, proper hits (19 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.18.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.17.0. `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` goes beside it, unchanged. Compiled in CI; not run here.

## Answers given
- **Levelling is identical:** the look switch only changes how a creature is drawn (its model, its name on screen, its portrait); the game engine never reads it. So Fernadillo is Bulbasaur underneath, with exactly the same stats, growth, moves, TMs, evolution levels, catch rate and experience. Switching mid-game changes nothing but the look.
- **HP:** the viewer shows *base* stats (Bulbasaur's base HP is 45). That number goes into the HP formula; a level 5 starter has 19 to 21 HP.

## Getting hit
The old hit was a slight lean. Now each one:
- is knocked back and up, flinches with its head snapping down and to one side, then shakes to settle;
- flattens its ears and opens its mouth;
- squeezes its eyes shut into a "> <" for a moment, using separate squint lids that only show on a hit.

How each family reacts:
- **Armadillos:** pull their heads in under their shells, then peek back out.
- **Quails:** ruffle their wings, flick their tails and plumes, and open their beaks.
- **Jerboas:** flail their arms and flatten their ears.
- **Goats and tapirs:** brace their front legs.

## Eyes
- **Smaller and shaped:** the corners are cut away and the lids have a crease, so the eyes are no longer square blocks.
- **The fierce ones:** narrow eyes, two texels tall, set just under the brows:
  - Brambadillo and Grovadon;
  - Emberam and Pyribex (amber);
  - Tsunapir (white, with the pupil to the front).
- **The young and prey ones:** still round, with the lower corner cut:
  - Fernadillo, Tidapir and Surfapir;
  - the quails;
  - the jerboas;
  - Cinderkid, now 2 by 3 rather than 3 by 3.

## Goat faces
- **Nose:** a soft leather nose pad across the muzzle's top, with a nostril each side, seen from the front and from above, and a split lip down the middle.
- **Mouth:** a mouth line along the bottom that runs back along each side of the muzzle.
- **Side tooth:** a white tooth shows at the side of the mouth on Emberam and Pyribex.
- **Lower teeth:** the front teeth show when the jaw opens.
- **Pyribex:** its square fang blocks are gone.

## Hooves
- **Cloven:** a heel, two toes in front with the cleft between them, and a dewclaw behind.
- **Paint:** a sheen down the front, and the bottom edge smouldering.

## Pyribex
Its tail flame is bigger: 12 wide and 19 tall, from four crossed planes.
