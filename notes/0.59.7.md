# Retromon 0.59.7: the PARTY PICKER, 2K photos, every gate a way through (2026-11-24)

John's 15:28 ask (a party from any point in the Kanto campaign, for videos), his 15:33 Kanto fly-through of 0.59.6, 15:35
(F6 pictures at 2K for the map) and 15:39 ("please deliver all this feedback"). Map jar unchanged (Retromon-Map-1.0.0).

## PARTY PICKER (15:28)
- **Where:** OPTIONS, the row before CONTROLS. Only in single player, or for an operator on a server.
- **Pick a point in Kanto:** STARTING OUT, after BROCK, MISTY, LT. SURGE, ERIKA, KOGA, SABRINA, BLAINE, GIOVANNI, and
  CHAMPION. Pick a STARTER too (Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle or Pikachu).
- **What you get:** six Retromon you could have by then.
  - The starter leads, a little ahead of the rest. The others come from what you can catch, be given or evolve by that point.
  - Evolutions and levels to match (after BROCK: lead 15-16, the rest 10-13; CHAMPION: lead 66-68, the rest 58-64).
  - Their moves are the best four they'd know at that level, not just the last four learned. HMs you'd have by then are
    taught (Cut from AFTER LT. SURGE, Fly from AFTER ERIKA, and so on), each in place of the weakest move.
  - It varies a little each time: R rolls again, A uses it. The same pick gives the same party on both sides.
- **CUSTOM:** your own six. Up and down pick the slot, left and right the species (shift for ten at a time), - and = the
  level, DELETE empties a slot. The mouse works too.
- **BADGES:** MATCH gives you the badges for that point (so HMs work in the field); KEEP MINE leaves yours.
- **MY OWN PARTY:** your real party and badges come back. The first time you pick, they're kept aside. Anything you
  caught while using a picked party stays with you; the picked ones go.
- Every species you're given is marked seen in the Pokédex.

## Photos at 2K (15:35)
- In PHOTO MODE (F6), **P takes a picture at 2560 x 1440**, whatever size the window is. For a moment the game draws at
  that size, then goes back.
- It's saved in the game's folder under `retromon_photos/`, named for the place and time, e.g.
  `pewter_city_20261124_153512.png`.
- It also becomes that place's TOWN MAP picture (the same as a postcard).
- PHOTO MODE no longer draws Lavender's night (15:33), so pictures there are in daylight.

## The gates (15:33: "All the gateways ... make sure that all of them are working and the way you go in and come out is honoured")
- **Johto's gatehouses:** 11 of them had no inside at all. Their dark doorway went nowhere, and the two by the NATIONAL
  PARK were the poor ones John tried. All 13 are now built as Kanto's gates are, with a guard at the desk.
  - **The 11 (from a scan of the map):** Route 29 to 46, Route 43's two, Violet to Route 31, Route 36, Route 32, the
    RUINS OF ALPH, Route 42, north of Ecruteak, Route 35 and Route 38.
  - **The NATIONAL PARK's two:** rebuilt, keeping their doors.
- **In and out:** you come in on the side you walked in from, facing in. You leave by the side you walk out of, facing
  away, past the gate's roof.
  - North and south gates: a mat each end, the north one's arrows pointing north.
  - West and east gates: a mat each side, pointing out.
  - North of Ecruteak has only its east side open, so it has one way.
- **Kanto's gates:** traced every room and door: every way out leads to a door in the world, and every door's way in
  stands inside its room.
- **Mats (15:33: "mats were aiming inwards instead of outwards"):** fixed:
  - the Cerulean houses' back doors;
  - the north passages in the Kanto gates and houses.

  A check of every mat in every room (126) found all of them now pointing out through their own doorway.
- **The floor (15:33: "not a big fan of the floor ... changed across the board"):** the blue check is gone from every
  gate, Kanto's and Johto's: pale stone flags instead. The INDIGO PLATEAU's gate keeps its own floor.
- **The Route 12 gate's plant (15:33):** the palm that stood at the foot of the stairs is gone. Gates without stairs
  keep it.
