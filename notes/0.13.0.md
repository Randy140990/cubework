# Retromon 0.13.0: the Pokédex in your hands, cap and bag, the world's Town Map (18 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.13.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.12.0, with `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` beside it (unchanged). Compiled in CI; not run here.

## The Pokédex in your hands
- **Opening (START):** you take it out of your pocket. Your figure dips its right hand to the pocket, brings the Pokédex up, the
  left hand joins, and you look down at it. Then it comes up the screen in your hands, over the world frosted behind it.
- **The device:** a red folding Pokédex, opened out, with:
  - a hinge down the middle, and the crease across its screen;
  - the blue lens and three lights top left, a speaker top right;
  - your forearms coming up from the bottom of the screen, your hands holding it from behind.

  The frost is Minecraft's menu blur (Options, Accessibility, Menu Background Blur); at 0 the world is just dimmed.
- **Its status bar:** the time, where you are and your money.
- **Closing (B):** it goes down, then you put it away.
- **What's on it:** the START menu, the Pokédex and the Town Map. CLOTHES opens the wardrobe (the device comes up again when you're
  done).
- **The world waits while it's out:**
  - the clock stops;
  - when playing alone, people and Pokémon stand still too;
  - you can't walk until it's put away.

## The START menu
- **Apps:** round buttons in two rows, as in the newer games: POKéDEX, POKéMON, BAG, MAP, CLOTHES, CARD, WAIT, SAVE, OPTION and
  EXIT.
  - The arrows move between them, A opens one, B puts the Pokédex away.
  - The picked button grows with a white ring, and its name shows on a cream tag.
- **Above and below them:** a hello with your badges and Pokédex count, and your party along the bottom with their HP.
- **BAG is your backpack, in your bag's colour.** Each pocket is part of the bag:
  - ITEMS: the big pocket;
  - BALLS: the front pocket with the zip;
  - KEY ITEMS: the lid with its buckles and Poké Ball badge;
  - TM & HM: the side pocket.

  Pick one (left and right, or click it): what's in it shows on the right, scrolling when there's a lot, with what the item under
  the cursor does.
- **CARD:** a proper trainer card with:
  - your face (from your look);
  - your name, ID number, money, Pokédex and time played;
  - the eight badges.
- **OPTION:** one line a setting (the speed key hint now says TAB).

## Your cap and bag, as models
- **The cap** is a 3D cap on your head. In CLOTHES:
  - **CAP STYLE:** POKé BALL, PIKACHU (with ears), BULBASAUR (bulb on top), CHARMANDER (a flame up the back), SQUIRTLE (its shell),
    EEVEE (ears and a fluffy band), GENGAR (spikes and a grin), SNORLAX, GREAT BALL, ULTRA BALL, or PLAIN in your hat colour.
  - **CAP:** FORWARDS or BACKWARDS.

  These two rows only matter with HAT set to CAP. The beanie and the sun hat are as before.
- **The bag**, in CLOTHES under BAG and BAG COLOUR:
  - **BACKPACK:** a pack with a lid, buckles, side pockets and straps over your shoulders;
  - **SATCHEL:** a bag at your hip on a strap across you;
  - **NONE.**
- **Defaults:**
  - the boy: the Poké Ball cap and a yellow backpack;
  - the girl: her sun hat and a yellow satchel.

  The skins you wear in game no longer have a cap or a pack painted on (`boy_bare.png`, `girl_bare.png`); the intro's figures
  are unchanged.
- **Where the gear shows:** on you in the world, in the wardrobe's mirror and on the battle stage. While the Pokédex is out, it's
  in your hands in the world too.

## The Town Map
- **MAP is the world itself, from above.** It's drawn from the Kanto map's own blocks, in the newer games' colours:
  - one soft grass green, cream paths and sand, deep green trees;
  - buildings in their own colours, outlined;
  - water paler where it's shallow, with a white line along the shore;
  - lit from the north-west, with short shadows from anything tall.
- **Moving around it:**
  - **zoom:** the mouse wheel or Q/E, from the whole land down to the houses of a town;
  - **move:** drag, or the arrows;
  - **back to you:** C;
  - **the other tabs:** Tab or click them. KANTO and JOHTO are the games' own Town Maps, every place on them, with FLY as
    before.
- **On the map:**
  - You're a red marker with an arrow showing which way you face.
  - North is on the compass.
  - A scale bar shows 10 to 1000 m (a block is a metre).
  - A zoom slider with + and - sits on the right.
- **It keeps up with what you build:** chunks around you are compared as they load, and anything changed since the map was baked
  is redrawn. The changes are kept in the save (`retromon_townmap.dat`).
- **Places on it:** the places marked with `/retromon zone` have their names on it.
  - **Labels:** towns always, red once you've been there; routes as you zoom in.
  - **Picking one** shows its card:
    - its picture, or "Not been here yet";
    - whether you've been, and "You are here.";
    - FLY HERE for a town you've been to (with FLY and the THUNDERBADGE).
  - **Pictures of places:** the first time you're in a place, the game takes its picture, once its name has gone and nothing's on
    screen (`retromon_places/<place>.png` in the save). No pictures to supply.
  - **Only marked places are named.** So far that's Pallet Town, Route 1, Viridian City, Route 22 and Route 2. Each place you mark
    also gets its music and wild Pokémon.
- **The Pokédex's ON MAP** shows the Pokémon's places glowing on MAP when they're marked there, or on KANTO or JOHTO when they
  aren't.
- **Baking:** the map was baked from the current Kanto world with these tools:
  - `tools/mapbake.py`: reads the region files;
  - `tools/mappalette.py`: block colours from the vanilla textures;
  - `tools/mapexport.py`: writes `assets/retromon/townmap/`.

  It opens on the land. The HeartGold & SoulSilver picture off the west coast is on the map but outside where it opens. When the
  world changes a lot, bake it again from the new world.
