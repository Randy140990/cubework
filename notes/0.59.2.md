# Retromon 0.59.2: all of Johto's outsides (2026-11-23, 19:17)

The map jar is unchanged (Retromon-Map-1.0.0).

I swept the whole Johto map for every block on the outside that still had its old look. The cliffside you're building west of Cianwood (Route 47/48) and the shore south of it are left alone for now; everything else, up to the beach, now has ours.

## Everywhere in Johto
- **Plain walls:** the white and grey of the Centers, Marts, houses, Mahogany's and Violet's buildings and the Lake's house are a clean render, with no flat vanilla concrete left. The quartz is clean white stone.
- **The gatehouses** (Routes 28, 29, 31, 32, 36, 42 and 43, and the ones by the NATIONAL PARK, Ilex, the BATTLE TOWER and Ecruteak):
  - the teal band is a teal trim;
  - the dark foot is dressed stone;
  - the signs are teal boards;
  - the mullions are the station's.
- **Doorway lights:** the bare sea lanterns and froglights in doorway floors are a warm cream light.
- **Trees:** every trunk is bark, as in Kanto. They were bare stripped spruce, including Routes 26 and 27's.
- **Trims:**
  - **Shelves:** every one is a flat board in its wood's colour. The signs stay signs.
  - **Diorite:** the trims are the houses' trim.
  - **Small pieces:** the iron bars are dark railings, and the barrels and bins are crates and bins.

## Flags (19:17: "unique in the different areas")
Each place's banners now have its own colours and designs, so the copies no longer match:
- **Ecruteak:** the towers' festival, deep red and orange cloths with gold flames, flowers and suns.
- **The NATIONAL PARK:** the Bug-Catching Contest's pennants, leaf green and lime, with a gold sun and diamonds.
- **Olivine:** the harbour's signal flags, navy and sky blue, with white waves, crosses and the lighthouse's gold light.
- **Cianwood:** the beach's, sunset orange and sea cyan, with the sun and the surf.
- **The S.S. AQUA:** dressed overall in signal flags, navy, white and red.

The poles are the dark lacquered poles from 0.59.0 in every place.
