# Retromon 0.27.1: the Pokémon Center and Poké Mart, tidied (20 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.27.1-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.27.0. `Retromon-Map-1.0.0.jar` hasn't changed: keep the one you have. Compiled in CI; not run here.

The Interior Viewer has every change below.

## Both shops
- **No signs inside:** POKéMON CENTER and POKé MART are gone from the walls.
- **The back corners are cut on the slant,** as the buildings are outside. Each one steps in half a block at a time, with black behind it.
- **Each room has its own door mat**, as carpet in its colours:
  - **The Center:** orange with a ball.
  - **The Mart:** blue with stripes.
  - **Oak's lab:** teal with a ball.
  - **Your house:** red with a ball.
  - **Daisy's house:** navy with stripes.

## Pokémon Center
- **Behind the counter:** the healing machine on the left, the nurse in the middle, and the PC on the far right with a big screen above it. The screen shows a heart beating over a ball.
- **On the counter:** a till at the right, and the bell in the middle.
- **Stairs up either side** to a landing and a dark way through, roped off. Look at them and you're told the 2F isn't open yet.
- **A waiting area each side:** a glass-topped table with flowers on it and two armchairs.
- **In the corners:** the world map and a bookshelf on the left, the storage PC and another bookshelf on the right. Low bookshelves and plants stand by the door.
- **A red runner** goes from the door to the counter.

## Poké Mart
- **The counter on the left,** with the till and a basket of balls. The clerk has a shelf of stock behind him.
- **The back wall:** two drinks coolers, a screen with an advert (a ball bouncing past a potion and a repel), a low stand of balls, and tall shelves.
- **Down the right:** two chest freezers with glass lids, then shelves of things for the road.
- **The middle:** a row of shelves facing the door, with a glass display case of balls in front of it.
- **A blue runner** goes from the door, and the floor is Let's Go's mint and white check.

## Maps are the world map, and open the Town Map
- **Every map inside is now your Kanto from above,** painted the way the Town Map paints it: the lab's map on the wall, the Center's map, and Daisy's Town Map on her table.
- **SPACE on any of them opens the Town Map.** B (or ESC) puts you back in the room.

## OAK's PARCEL
- **The clerk calls you over** from behind his counter when you come into Viridian's Mart, then gives you the parcel. He no longer tries to walk round the counter.
- **The nurse and clerk who stood on the roofs** are taken away. That's now checked every couple of seconds, so they can't come back.
