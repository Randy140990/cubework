# Retromon 0.22.0: your house, the rival's house and Oak's lab inside; new people (19 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.22.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.21.0. `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` goes beside it, unchanged. Compiled in CI; not run here.

This pass brings the first three interiors into the mod and repaints everyone. Your Kanto stays exactly as it is: the mod adds no outside blocks. The UI Viewer's new screens come into the mod screen by screen in the next updates.

## Interiors
- **A space of their own:** the insides live in their own dimension (`retromon:interiors`), built from the Interior Viewer's rooms. They aren't built inside your houses, so the map jar stays separate.
  - Same layouts, sizes and textures as the viewer, at 16 pixels a block, with the squared-wood floors.
  - The models are the viewer's, given a slightly blockier feel.
- **Doors in your world lead in:**
  - Your house's double door takes you downstairs into your house.
  - The rival's door takes you into his house.
  - The lab's copper door takes you into the lab.
  - The mat inside takes you back out, just south of the door you came in by.
  - Every move between inside and out fades through.
- **Upstairs:** the stairs in both houses take you up and down.
- **The camera** is the same as outside, and the same as the viewer's Game camera. Talking to someone eases into the close-up with the cinema bars.
- **People inside:**
  - Mum is in your kitchen and Daisy in the rival's house.
  - Prof. Oak and his aides are in the lab: the woman at the top, the one at the desk, and the two by the right-hand shelves.
  - The aides from the old games say their own lines.
- **Lab:** white, full shelves, the desk and whiteboard in the bottom-left corner, the incubator under the top-left desk, and the second shelf row on the right.

## A new game
1. **NEW GAME** starts you in your bedroom, upstairs.
2. Go down the stairs and out of the front door into Pallet Town.
3. **Oak stops you** at the tall grass and takes you to his lab: the screen fades and you're inside.
4. **Choose your starter** in the lab, then your rival picks his and battles you there.
5. **You can't leave the lab before choosing.** Oak calls you back from the door.

## People
- **Every skin repainted** (`tools/skins2.py`):
  - **Shading:** clothes are lit from the top and front, darker down the sides, the back and the shadowed inside of the arms, with fades top to bottom.
  - **Clothes:** folds, seams, cuffs, collars, hems, pockets and soles.
  - **Faces:** whites of the eyes, irises, brows, a nose shadow and a mouth.
  - **Outer layer:** hair stands off the head for fringes, spikes, buns, ponytails and long hair down the back. Coats, jackets, aprons and skirts stand off the body.
  - **Sleeves now cover the tops of the arms**, on the people and on your own jacket, tee and hoodie.
- **Named roles in our own looks:** these are our own designs, not the games' or the show's, and the Person Viewer marks them "Our own look". The names and lines stay as the games' for now.
  - Prof. Oak, Blue, the nurse and the officer.
  - The villain team, with its own mark: a cut ring.
  - The eight gym leaders and the Elite Four.
  - The story's helpers.
