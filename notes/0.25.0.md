# Retromon 0.25.0: John's Pallet Town test (19 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.25.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.24.1. `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` goes beside it, unchanged. Compiled in CI; not run here.

## Lag, and the lines through the boxes
- **The cause of both:** every rounded corner was drawn pixel by pixel, so one panel was tens of thousands of tiny squares each frame (the menu, the battle and the Pokédex were the worst). The panels' shading was also laid over the glass a second time, which left a line across part way down (the battle plates, the Next panel, the name list).
- **Fixed:** corners, circles and ellipses are now drawn from smooth textures, a few pieces each, and each panel's glass is drawn once.
- **The battle buttons** no longer show notches at their ends: each button's face is one shape.

## The menus
- **The START menu, the Pokédex and the Town Map** are full-window pages over the frosted world, as in the UI Viewer. There's no device in your hands any more, and no night backdrop behind the menu tiles.
- **Clicking works** on every page, Pokédex rows included.
- **Every key hint is a button** (SPACE Choose, ESC Back, TAB...), so there's always something to click to go back or close.

## The start
- **INSID3 TH3 CUB3 presents** plays every time the title comes up: at the start (once Minecraft's loading screen has gone) and after you quit a game.
- **DELETE on a save** now asks to delete it, instead of loading the game.
- **The professor's scene:** the little walker goes round Oak in a wide loop, clear of the others, facing the way it walks. It walked backwards before. (Fixed in the UI Viewer too.)
- **The character maker** has more room: wider label column, more space between rows and cards, and labels spaced as the viewer's, so COLOUR no longer runs into the swatches.
- **Your cap and bag show in the maker** and on the stands (they were only drawn in the world, so the boy looked bare). The boy starts in the Person Viewer's P-001 look: the cap, jacket and backpack. MIX IT UP really does change everything (it gave you the satchel and no cap last time).
- **Off you go:** everything fades to black, the loading page fades in, and the world fades in from black when you arrive.

## In the world
- **Minecraft's keys are off** in the Retromon game: E no longer opens the inventory, and the hotbar keys, Q, F, T, F3, F5 and other mods' keys (shaders, maps, schematics) do nothing out in the world. Walking, running, SPACE, ENTER, TAB, Esc, F1, F2 and F11 work.
- **Who's talking:** people's names are on the text box's tab (MUM, DAISY...).
- **The talk prompt:** a small SPACE Talk (or SPACE Look) pill floats over whoever or whatever SPACE would talk to, as in the Interior Viewer. (The Interior Viewer now shows SPACE too, not E.)
- **Talking to someone:** the camera eases in and out smoothly. It jumped before, faster the higher your frame rate.
- **Inside:** going into a building shows its name in the banner (PROF. OAK's LAB, YOUR HOUSE, BLUE's HOUSE). Music plays inside: the lab's own music in the lab, Pallet Town's in the houses. Pallet Town's comes back when you go out.
- **People's corners are softened**, as the Person Viewer draws them: everyone in the world, you included (in a Retromon save), and the figures in the menus.
- **POKéMON LOOK starts on RETROMON** (our creatures). It's switched over once for existing settings too. It's still in OPTION.

## Your map
- **Nothing of ours on the map any more.** The mod had been putting two shelves wherever the old games have a sign, and faint light round lamps. Neither is put down now, and the ones already there are taken away the first time each place loads.
- **Your own shelf signs are left alone:** a pair is only taken away where it's the only shelves near the sign's spot and stands exactly where the mod put it. What was taken away is listed in your world's folder (retromon_removed_signs.txt), in case one was yours.

## Inside buildings
- **Their own light:** the insides no longer get sunlight (no sunset shadows indoors). They're lit by their own lamps whatever the time.
- **Oak's lab:** the balls on the table and in the incubator now have a red top seen from above, so they no longer look like two stacked. The incubator's round parts no longer flicker.

## Battle
- **Fainting:** it falls over and lies there a moment. Then its trainer throws a ball beside it, the ball opens, red light draws it in, and the ball goes back to the hand. Wild ones fade away where they lie. Before, it shrank away while still falling.
- **Calling one back** (switching) works the same way, with the thrown ball.

## OPTION
- **SHADERS:** ON (Tempered Light) or OFF (Minecraft's own light). It changes straight away, so you can feel how much of the lag is the shader.
- **Smaller rows** so all twelve fit.

## Not in this jar
- **Our own lighting** instead of Tempered Light: waiting on your choice (see the message).
- **The title's OPTIONS** still opens Minecraft's options.
- **LANGUAGE** (English only so far).

## Good to know
- **POKé BALLS:** as in Red and Blue, Oak gives you five after you've taken him his parcel from Viridian City's Mart. Until then there's nothing to catch with.
