# Retromon 0.12.0: Pokémon stand right, prize money (18 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.12.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.11.0, with `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` beside it (unchanged). Compiled in CI; not run here.

## The models
Most of what looked wrong in the model check came from Retromon, not from Cobblemon's models.
- **Joints were turned the wrong way.** Cobblemon's models are in Bedrock's handedness and Minecraft's model parts are the other
  way round. We flipped all three turns when only the last one (the roll) should be flipped. Every bone, every cube and every
  animation frame was bent backwards. That caused:
  - Pikachu, Sandshrew and Sandslash covering their eyes;
  - Onix on its head, and Arcanine upside down;
  - Pidgey, Spearow and Fearow folded into boxes;
  - Ekans and Arbok not snake-shaped;
  - arms up over the head (Electabuzz, Magmar, Tauros, Hitmonlee, Hitmonchan);
  - Mr. Mime's arms out of his head, Rhydon's horn down;
  - Doduo and Dodrio's heads merged, and Magneton's magnets through its face.
- **Wrong poses were picked.** Some Pokémon stood in their sleep pose (Arcanine, Hypno, Gloom). Some used a flying idle on the
  ground (Charizard, Gyarados). Some walked with a ride or jump animation. Now:
  - battles use the battle stand;
  - the world uses the normal stand;
  - walking, flying and hovering use their own loops.
- Party and Pokédex pictures are redrawn, and the model check page has the new pictures.
- Not changed yet: shapes and sizes that are Cobblemon's own design (Metapod, the Nidorans, Golem and Gyarados sizes). Those come
  after you've looked at the redrawn page.

## Money
- **Beating a trainer:**
  - "PLAYER got ₽X for winning!" (their rate times their last Pokémon's level; your rival's first battle pays ₽175);
  - a MONEY panel slides in at the top left of the battle, counting up to your new total, with a cash register sound.
- **Pay Day:** "PLAYER picked up ₽X!" at the end, counted on top.
- **Blacking out:** half your money goes, as in the games, and the battle now says so:
  - against a trainer: "PLAYER paid ₽X to the winner.";
  - in the wild: "PLAYER panicked and dropped ₽X...".

  Losing your rival's first two battles still costs nothing, and no longer says "blacked out".
- **Out in the world:** when your money changes, a gold panel slides in at the top right with the total and the change (+₽175).
- **The menu:** shows your MONEY under the list.
- Money is shown as ₽3,000 everywhere (the menu, the TRAINER CARD, the POKé MART, what people say), not ¥.
- Money tops out at ₽999,999.
