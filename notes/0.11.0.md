# Retromon 0.11.0: three save slots (18 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.11.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.10.0, with `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` beside it (unchanged). Compiled in CI; not run here.

## Save slots
- Up to three games: SLOT 1, 2 and 3 are the saves called Retromon, Retromon 2 and Retromon 3 (the names new games always had).
- **CONTINUE** shows the three side by side, each with:
  - a picture of where you are (taken a few seconds in, every half minute after while you're out in the world with nothing open,
    and after you SAVE; the middle of the screen, clear of the HUD),
  - your name (blue for the boy, pink for the girl) and where you are,
  - eight badge dots (gold for each one won),
  - POKéDEX caught (or NO POKéDEX YET) and time played,
  - your party's pictures, and when you last played.
  An empty slot says EMPTY; picking it starts a new game there.
- **NEW GAME** goes into the first empty slot. With all three full it asks which one to start over in, then asks again
  ("Start over in SLOT 2?", NO picked to begin with). The old game isn't deleted: it's moved to `.minecraft/retromon-old-saves`,
  where the newest three are kept.
- Games in saves called Retromon 4 and up (from before the limit) aren't shown; they're still in your saves folder (MINECRAFT >
  Singleplayer opens them, or delete them there).
- Older saves show their picture from Minecraft's own world icon and their name from the save until they've been played once.
- Saving itself is unchanged: Minecraft saves the world (every few minutes, on SAVE, and when you leave), with your Pokémon,
  bag and progress in it.

## Also
- Time played is counted (real time, whatever the game speed).
- Ditto was invisible in game: its own body is called "ditto", which was being hidden along with the Ditto faces other models
  carry. It shows now (and in its party picture).
- `tools/gallery.py` draws the first 151 as the game draws them, front and back, for the model check page.
