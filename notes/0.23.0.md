# Retromon 0.23.0: the UI Viewer's screens in the game (19 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.23.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.22.1. `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` goes beside it, unchanged. Compiled in CI; not run here.

This pass brings in the UI Viewer's screens for everything you meet in Pallet Town. Every screen is drawn as the viewer draws it: the viewer's 1280 by 720 layout is fitted to your window (as large as it goes, centred), with its panels, pills, keys, text sizes, colours and pictures. The backdrops fill the whole window.

## The start
- **Title:** RETROMON's blocky word over the blocky hill with our creatures and moving clouds (the Retromon look). With the Cobblemon look it's POKéMON over Charizard hovering at night. PRESS START blinks.
- **Menu and saves:** the menu is the viewer's list over the title. Your saves are cards (picture, name, place, Pokédex, badges, play time, DELETE), with you on a stand beside them.
- **Oak's welcome:**
  - Oak stands on the glowing floor with our creatures about him. One wanders in a loop, the bird hovers flapping, and now and then one calls out.
  - At "Some people keep them as friends", the little yellow one jumps up onto his shoulder.
  - He talks with his hands.
- **The character maker:** boy or girl, eight skin tones (four new), eye colour (new), hair and its colour, then your clothes. It's laid out in cards, two columns, with THAT'S ME and MIX IT UP, and you turn on a stand beside it. What you make is your look in the game.
- **Names:** the ready names in a list. NEW NAME opens the viewer's letters: type on the keyboard, or move round them.
- **Your rival:** Oak's grandson beside him while you pick his name.
- **Off you go:** you wave beside Oak, then the loading screen (a ball spinning, PALLET TOWN, the bar, a tip).

## In the world
- **Talking:** the viewer's text box everywhere. The speaker sits on a cream tab ("OAK:" comes off the words and goes there), names show in gold, and the mint arrow bobs when there's more. Questions are answered in the viewer's list.
- **HUD:**
  - The keys along the bottom right (SPACE Talk, ENTER Menu, TAB Speed).
  - The time at the bottom left.
  - **Next** at the top right: "Go to PROF. OAK's lab" and how far, until you've a POKéMON.
  - The place banner slides in with the town's line from its sign.
  - Money changes pop up as a pill.
- **Doors:** Minecraft's "Loading terrain" screen no longer shows. The trip is just the door's fade to dark and back.

## Oak's lab and the battle
- Oak's starter scene uses the viewer's text box and YES/NO list.
- **Nicknames:** the viewer's question, then its letters.
- **Battle:**
  - "BLUE wants to battle!" slides his card in with speed lines.
  - The plates are the viewer's: name, level, HP bar, HP and EXP for yours, the party's balls above.
  - The four commands are the stack of buttons with their pictures and the arrow, with "What will ... do?" above them.
  - The moves are the viewer's pills, the picked one cream. Above them, a panel shows its type, kind, how it'll do, power, accuracy and what it does.
  - What's said is in the viewer's text box beside your plate.
- **Catching:** "Press SPACE or click to throw!".

## START menu (on your device)
- **Menu:** the viewer's ten round tiles in two rows, our own symbols, what each does underneath.
- **POKéMON:** six cards, three across: picture, name, level, types, status, HP, FOLLOWING YOU on your partner. SUMMARY, SWITCH, FOLLOW ME and CANCEL.
- **Summary:** the creature in 3D on a stand. INFO, STATS (as bars) and MOVES pages; TAB or left and right turns the page.
- **Bag:** your backpack in its colour with its four pockets (the picked one lifted and outlined). The items are listed with their pictures and how many, and what each does underneath.
- **Town Map:** cliffs in browns and the island off the north coast left off. The place panel on the right has the town's line, its picture, its **gym** (leader, type, our badge, levels), the wild POKéMON and FLY HERE.
- **Trainer card:** you on a stand, your details, and the **eight badges of our own**.
- **Clothes:** the maker's clothes cards, with UNDO and MIX IT UP, you on a stand beside them. B keeps what you chose.
- **Option:** each row's value between arrows, and what it does in the panel above. POKéMON LOOK switches between Retromon and Cobblemon.
