# Retromon 0.15.0: Retromon's own creatures, a first test (18 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.15.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.14.0, with `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` beside it (unchanged). Compiled in CI; not run here.

## POKéMON LOOK (START, OPTION, last row)
- **COBBLEMON** (the default): as before.
- **RETROMON:** the first five you meet are Retromon's own creatures, with their own names on every screen.
  - **What changes:** their models and names only.
  - **What stays the same:** underneath they're the same species. Stats, moves, types, levelling, evolution, cries and
    music are untouched.
- **The switch is live:** in the world, in battle, in the Pokédex and on the party pictures, the moment you change it.

## The five
| In place of | Retromon's own | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bulbasaur | **FERNADILLO** | A little armadillo armoured in overlapping leaves, its tail a curled fern frond. It ducks its head under its leaves when hit. |
| Charmander | **CINDERKID** | A young goat with charcoal wool, ember horns, an ember fringe and a flame of a tail. Its hooves and horns glow, and the glow flickers. It bleats and rears. |
| Squirtle | **TIDAPIR** | A young tapir, sea blue, with pale wave stripes, round ears with pale tips and a fin along its back. It trumpets with its trunk up and stamps. |
| Pidgey | **QUAILET** | A round lilac-grey quail with a cream scalloped breast, white face stripes and a dark teardrop plume. It flies with barred wings. |
| Rattata | **JERBIT** | A sandy jerboa with huge pink-lined ears, long hind feet it hops on, and a long tail with a black and white tuft. |

- **Each has its own animations**, none taken from anyone else's:
  - standing, a livelier battle stance, walking (Jerbit hops) and calling out;
  - getting hit, and blinking;
  - flying and hovering for Quailet.
- **Sizes:** each stands about as tall as the model it stands in for.

## How they're made
- **`tools/mons.py`** builds them from scratch:
  - the geometry, cube by cube;
  - the textures, painted in 3D, every texel worked out from where it sits on the model;
  - the animations.

  It writes them to `assets/retromon/mons/<species>/` (model, animations and info) and `textures/mons/`, with their names in
  `mons/names.json`.
- **`tools/monshow.py`** makes:
  - their pictures for the screens (`textures/gui/mon_r/`);
  - the line-up and the film of them moving.

## Not in this one
