# Retromon 0.26.0: Retromon Light, and Retromon Map (20 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.26.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.25.0. Compiled in CI; not run here.

## The map jar has a new name: Retromon-Map-1.0.0.jar
- Take `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` out of your mods folder and put `Retromon-Map-1.0.0.jar` in its place.
- It's the same map inside. It's now called Retromon Map in the mods list.
- It doesn't depend on a Minecraft version, so the same jar goes beside any Retromon.
- If the old one is still there, Retromon still finds it, but you only need the new one.

## Retromon Light (OPTION > SHADERS)
Our own shader pack, built light. It needs Iris, the same as before.

**SHADERS now has three settings:**
- **RETROMON (the default):** Retromon Light. If you had SHADERS on before, you're now on this.
- **TEMPERED:** Tempered Light, unchanged from 0.25.0, to fall back on.
- **OFF:** Minecraft's own light.

**Outside:**
- The sun moves with the time of day, and the shadows move with it. Their edges are soft.
- Morning and evening light is warm, and the sky glows orange round the low sun.
- Nights are a clear blue (as in Legends Z-A), not black. OPTION's NIGHTS still sets how bright they are.
- Lamps and torches give a warm light, and rain dims the day.
- There's no blur, no glare and no brightness that changes as you look about, so it's quick. Coming out of a building, it's already as light or dark as the time says.

**Inside buildings:**
- The room is lit by its own light, the same at any time of day. No sun moves across the room and nothing changes at sunset.
- Rooms lit by their lamps are evenly bright. A room without a light stays dark.
- All round the room is black.

**The pack's own settings** (Iris > Shader Packs > Retromon Light > Settings): Sunlight, Night, Shadow softness, Shadow depth, Inside, Shadow detail, Shadow distance, Brightness, Colour and Darker corners.

## To check
- **Outside:** in Pallet Town, try morning, midday, sunset and night (WAIT in the START menu moves the time on). Look at how the shadows move.
- **Inside:** go into your house and Oak's lab at sunset and at night. The light should stay the same as at midday.
- **The setting:** START > OPTION > SHADERS: try RETROMON, TEMPERED and OFF. Each one switches straight away.
- **Speed:** compare RETROMON with TEMPERED.
