# Retromon 0.8.0: wild Pokémon in the grass, your Pokémon walks with you, a win to celebrate (18 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.8.0-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.7.1. The map jar is unchanged. Saves carry on. Compiled in CI; not run here.

## Wild Pokémon you can see (as Let's Go)
- No more random battles in the grass: wild Pokémon appear in it within about 10 blocks of you (rustling out of the grass), up to 4 at a time, and walk about in it. Walk into one to battle exactly that one.
- They're the place's own: picked from its wild list (Yellow for Kanto, Gold and Silver for Johto) by the time of day, at the games' levels, rare ones less often.
- Flying types (Pidgey, Spearow and the rest) take to the air now and then and fly over the grass, wings open, with Cobblemon's own fly and hover animations. The others walk with their walk animations.
- They wander off and go when you're more than about 22 blocks away (or after a while out of reach).
- In caves (and other places where any step can have one) they walk about the floor.
- A REPEL keeps away the ones weaker than your lead.
- OPTION > WILD POKéMON: SEEN (default) or HIDDEN (the classic random battles).

## Your Pokémon walks with you
- Your first Pokémon walks a step behind you (as in Yellow and the newer games), with its walk animation. It catches up if it falls back, and pops back to you if it gets left far behind. Talk to it (SPACE) to see how it's feeling (tired, happy, overjoyed, by its friendship).
- POKéMON > pick one > FOLLOW ME: that one walks with you instead (kept in your save).
- It steps aside for battles and comes back after.
- OPTION > WALK WITH YOU: ON (default) or OFF.

## A win
- When you win a battle, your Pokémon celebrates: the camera comes round in front of it as it hops twice for joy (a twirl on the first), with its cry, sparkles and notes, as the victory music starts.

## Townsfolk
- People who walk about in the games stroll a few steps round their spot (some only up and down, or left and right, as in the games). People who stand still look about now and then. They stop when you talk to them.

## Signs
- Reading a sign (or a shelf standing in for one) now gives the words of the games' sign nearest that spot: "PALLET TOWN / Shades of your journey await!", "OAK POKéMON RESEARCH LAB", "{your name}'s house", TRAINER TIPS, the Mart and Pokémon Center signs, the gym signs (194 signs from Yellow, `tools/signs.py`). A sign with its own words keeps them.

## Models
- Cobblemon's walk, fly and hover poses are brought in (`tools/assets.py`), each with its own parts shown (Pidgey's wings open to fly, folded to walk).
