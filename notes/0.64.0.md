## Outside, and in your caves, forests, Safari Zone and Mt. Silver: on the spot
A battle there is fought where you are, in your build as it is.
- **The layout** is the same as before:
  - you behind your POKéMON;
  - theirs across the field, their trainer behind it;
  - the camera behind yours, a little above, looking across.
- **The way it faces:** towards the trainer who's battling you, or the way you're facing for a wild one. It can lie
  along any of eight ways (the diagonals are for the caves' slanting tunnels). It only turns another way where that's
  clearly more open.
- **Cleared on your screen only**, and put back when the battle ends. Only the strip of world the battle uses is
  cleared: 10 blocks across, from behind their trainer to behind the camera. In it:
  - grass, flowers, ferns, bushes and the like;
  - leaves;
  - fences, walls, signs, lamp posts and the like.
- **Never cut:** anything solid (the ground, rock, buildings, tree trunks, a cave's walls). Where something solid
  would be in the field or between the camera and the POKéMON:
  - the battle moves to the nearest open spot (up to 6 blocks away outside, 10 in a cave or a forest, twice that if
    nowhere is open that close), or
  - the camera comes in closer.
  It also keeps the field on the ground at your height (a bridge's deck, not the water beside it).
- **Everyone else** near the field (people, wandering POKéMON) steps out of the picture.
- **The world beyond** is your map, all the way to the horizon.
- **At night**, a few unseen lamps hang in the air over the field (only where there's nothing).
- **Surfing:** theirs floats on the water, and a SWIMMER is swimming.
- **Evolving outside a battle** happens on the spot too.

  - VIRIDIAN FOREST, ILEX FOREST and the SAFARI ZONE.
- **Each opens on the new Battle camera** (the game's own: its place, its 52 degrees). Its panel says what was

- **Its blocks:**

  - the Retromon Look pack's pictures where it has the block;
  - otherwise a plain picture in the block's colours.
  In the game it's your world and its real textures, with the horizon; the viewer has the blocks round the battle.
