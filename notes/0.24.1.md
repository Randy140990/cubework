# Retromon 0.24.1: BATTLE STYLE (19 Nov 2026)

`Retromon-0.24.1-for-MC1.21.11.jar` replaces 0.24.0. `retromon-kanto-map-1.0.0.jar` goes beside it, unchanged. Compiled in CI; not run here. Everything from 0.24.0 is in it (see its notes).

## BATTLE STYLE (START > OPTION)
- **SWITCH (the default):** when you knock out a trainer's POKéMON and they've another, you're asked "BLUE is about to use PIDGEY. Will you change POKéMON?".
  - **YES:** pick one from your team. Yours is called back, the new one is thrown out, and then the trainer throws out their next one.
  - **NO,** or ESC: you keep the one that's out, and the trainer sends theirs.
  - You're only asked if you've another POKéMON that can fight.
- **SET:** no question; the trainer just sends out their next one.

## Calling back and sending out (as in the Creature Viewer)
- **Calling one back:** it's tinted red and shrinks as red light streams from it into its trainer's hand. This happens whenever you switch, not only with BATTLE STYLE.
- **Sending one out:** the ball lands and pops open in a flash of white light, and the POKéMON comes out glowing white. This is the same for yours and for the trainer's.

## To check
- With two or more POKéMON, beat a trainer who has two (for example, a trainer on Route 22 or in Viridian Forest), and try YES, NO and ESC at the question.
- Switch to SET under OPTION and battle again: no question.
- The rival battle in the lab is one on one, so the question doesn't come up there.
